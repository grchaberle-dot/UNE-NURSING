# Acute Cardiac Conditions

## Key Terms
Contractility, stroke volume, cardiac output, preload, afterload, ejection fraction

## APO (Acute Pulmonary Oedema)
Cardiogenic (LV failure) vs Non-cardiogenic. Treatment: frusemide, high-flow O2, GTN, morphine

## Arrhythmias
AF, atrial flutter, SVT, VT, VF. Normal HR 60-100, tachy >100, brady <60

## ECG Interpretation
Rate → Regularity → QRS morphology → P wave → PR interval → T wave → ST segment
