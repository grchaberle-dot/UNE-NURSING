# Anaesthesia & Perioperative Nursing

## Classifications
General, Local, Conscious sedation, Regional

## 4 Stages
1. Analgesia (Entonox)
2. Delirium (move through quickly)
3. Surgical anaesthesia (muscle relaxation incl resp)
4. Medullary paralysis (resp failure)

## Key Drugs
- Propofol: widely used, short procedures
- Ketamine: dissociative at low doses
- Antimuscarinics: MOST IMPORTANT pre-med (reduce secretions, reduce aspiration risk)
- Lignocaine: most common local anaesthetic
- EMLA: prilocaine + lignocaine, paediatric use
