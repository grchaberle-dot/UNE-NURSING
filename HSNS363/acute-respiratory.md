# Acute Respiratory Conditions

## CXR Interpretation (RIP-ABCDE)
Rotation, Inspiration, Penetration, Airway, Bones, Cardiac silhouette, Diaphragm, Everything else

## Pneumonia
Alveoli fill with fluid/pus. Types: bacterial, viral, mycoplasma. Treatment: antibiotics

## Anaphylaxis
Adrenaline IM 0.5mg 3-5 minutely (max 2mg). Hartmann's 1000mL IV if shocked

## ARDS
Fluid in alveoli preventing O2 exchange. Phases: exudative → proliferative → fibrotic
