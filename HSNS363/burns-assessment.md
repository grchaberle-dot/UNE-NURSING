# Burns Assessment & Management

## Depth
- Superficial: epidermis only, red, painful
- Partial thickness superficial: blistered, very painful
- Partial thickness deep: less pain (nerve damage)
- Full thickness: white/brown/black, painless, needs grafting

## Rule of Nines (Adult)
Head 9%, each arm 9%, anterior trunk 18%, posterior trunk 18%, each leg 18%, perineum 1%

## Management Priorities
1. Stop burning, cool 20 min running water
2. Airway (suspect inhalation injury)
3. Fluid resuscitation: Parkland formula 4mL x kg x %TBSA
4. Pain, infection prevention, wound care

## Phases
Emergent (0-48hr) → Acute (wound care) → Rehabilitation
