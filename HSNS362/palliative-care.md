# Palliative Care Nursing

Improves quality of life for patients/families with life-limiting illness. Not restricted to final days.

## Preferred Route
Subcutaneous when oral not possible (CADD driver).

## Three Bs
Bowels (constipation), Barfing (nausea), Breakthrough pain (rescue doses)

## WHO Pain Ladder
1. Non-opioid +/- adjuvant
2. Weak opioid
3. Strong opioid

## Voluntary Assisted Dying
Legal in all Australian states + ACT. Strict eligibility and safeguards.
