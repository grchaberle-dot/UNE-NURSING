# Advanced Care Planning & End of Life

## Advance Care Directives
Legally binding under common law if made by competent person. Cannot instruct assisted dying.

## Nurse's Role
Recognise if ACD exists, communicate at handover, initiate conversation if needed.

## Signs of Actively Dying
Decreased urine, weight loss, breathing changes, skin colour changes, decreased eating, BP drop, cold extremities, confusion, increased sleeping.

## Nurse Advocacy
Listen, respect wishes, individualise comfort, maintain dignity.
