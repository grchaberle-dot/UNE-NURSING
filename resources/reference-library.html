Key Nursing Texts by Topic | APA 7th Format | Assessment Ready

These references come from real UNE BNURS student assessments and unit materials. Use them as starting points for your own research. Always verify currency and check your unit guide for any required texts. All formatted in APA 7th.

\uD83C\uDFE5 Core Nursing Textbooks

Brown, D., & Edwards, H. (2005). Lewis's Medical-Surgical Nursing: Assessment and Management of Clinical Problems. Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

The go-to for med-surg nursing. Assessment frameworks, clinical problems, nursing interventions. Referenced in nearly every UNE assessment.

Bullock, S., & Hales, M. (2019). Principles of Pathophysiology (2nd ed.). Melbourne: Pearson Australia.

Disease processes, pathophysiology of all body systems. Essential for understanding WHY patients present the way they do.

Foster, K., Marks, P., O'Brien, A., & Raeburn, T. (Eds.). (2021). Mental Health in Nursing: Theory and Practice for Clinical Settings (5th ed.). Elsevier.

Mental health assessment, therapeutic relationships, interventions, specific disorders. Core text for MH units.

Crisp, J., Douglas, C., Rebeiro, G., & Waters, D. (Eds.). (2021). Potter and Perry's Fundamentals of Nursing (6th ed.). Elsevier Australia.

Nursing fundamentals: assessment, ADPIE, documentation, clinical skills, patient safety. Your first-year bible.

\uD83D\uDC8A Pharmacology

Tiziani, A. (2006). Harvard's Nursing Guide to Drugs (7th ed.). Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

Drug reference: actions, indications, contraindications, side effects, nursing considerations. Check for newer editions.

Bryant, B., & Knight, K. (2007). Pharmacology for Health Professionals (2nd ed.). Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

Broader pharmacology context. Drug classes, mechanisms of action, clinical applications.

MIMS Australia. (2025). MIMS Online. Retrieved from https://www.mims.com.au/

Current Australian drug database. Dosing, interactions, consumer info. Always check here for up-to-date prescribing info.

\uD83E\uDDE0 Mental Health

Meadows, G., Singh, B., & Grigg, M. (Eds.). (2012). Mental Health in Australia (2nd ed.). Oxford University Press.

Australian MH context, assessment frameworks, service delivery, cultural considerations.

Weiner, L., Ossola, P., Causin, J. B., et al. (2019). Racing thoughts revisited. Journal of Affective Disorders, 255, 69-76.

Explains pressure of speech, flight of ideas, racing thoughts in mania. Useful for mental state examination discussions.

\u2764\uFE0F Cardiac & Emergency

Curtis, K., Ramsden, C., & Friendship, J. (2007). Emergency & Trauma Nursing. Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

Emergency assessment (ABC, GCS), stroke care, trauma management, triage.

Elliot, D., Aitken, L., & Chaboyer, W. (2007). ACCCN's Critical Care Nursing. Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

ICU nursing, hemodynamic monitoring, ventilation, critical care pharmacology.

Porth, C. (2005). Pathophysiology: Concepts of Altered Health States (7th ed.). Philadelphia: Lippincott Williams & Wilkins.

Deep pathophysiology. Cardiac, respiratory, neuro, renal. The heavy hitter for understanding disease processes.

\uD83D\uDC76 Paediatrics & Respiratory

Children's Health Queensland Hospital and Health Service. (2022). Queensland Paediatric Emergency Care Nursing Skill Sheets: How Children Are Different.

Anatomical and physiological differences in children. Essential for paediatric respiratory assessment (smaller airways, higher RR, accessory muscle use).

National Asthma Council [NAC]. (2025). Asthma. https://www.nationalasthma.org.au/

Australian asthma guidelines, action plans, medication info. The authoritative source for asthma management in Australia.

Royal Children's Hospital Melbourne [RCHM]. (2023). Clinical Practice Guidelines.

Paediatric clinical guidelines: asthma, respiratory, medication dosing for children. Free online.

\uD83D\uDCCB Clinical Standards & Guidelines

Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care [ACSQHC]. (2021). National Safety and Quality Health Service Standards (2nd ed.).

The 8 national standards. Comprehensive care, medication safety, clinical governance. Referenced in nearly every assessment about quality and safety.

Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia [NMBA]. (2016). Registered Nurse Standards for Practice.

What's expected of you as a registered nurse. Scope of practice, professional conduct, clinical competence.

Joanna Briggs Institute. (2008). Pressure ulcers: prevention of pressure related damage. Best Practice: Evidence Based Information Sheets for Health Professionals, 12(2), 1-4.

Evidence-based practice guidelines. JBI is a key source for systematic reviews in nursing.
