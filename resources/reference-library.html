Key Nursing Texts by Topic | APA 7th Format | Assessment Ready

These references come from real UNE BNURS student assessments and unit materials. Use them as starting points for your own research. Always verify currency and check your unit guide for required texts. All in APA 7th.

\uD83C\uDFE5 Core Nursing Textbooks

Brown, D., & Edwards, H. (2005). Lewis's Medical-Surgical Nursing: Assessment and Management of Clinical Problems. Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

The go-to for med-surg. Assessment frameworks, clinical problems, interventions. Referenced in nearly every UNE assessment.

Bullock, S., & Hales, M. (2019). Principles of Pathophysiology (2nd ed.). Melbourne: Pearson Australia.

Disease processes across all body systems. Essential for understanding WHY patients present the way they do.

Crisp, J., Douglas, C., Rebeiro, G., & Waters, D. (Eds.). (2021). Potter and Perry's Fundamentals of Nursing (6th ed.). Elsevier Australia.

Nursing fundamentals: ADPIE, documentation, clinical skills, patient safety. First-year bible.

Fosbre, C. (2022). Varcarolis Essentials of Psychiatric Mental Health Nursing: A Communication Approach to Evidence-Based Care (5th ed.). Elsevier.

Psychiatric nursing communication, therapeutic relationships, mental health assessment. Excellent for MH placement prep.

\uD83D\uDC8A Pharmacology

Tiziani, A. (2006). Harvard's Nursing Guide to Drugs (7th ed.). Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

Drug reference: actions, indications, contraindications, side effects, nursing considerations.

Bryant, B., & Knight, K. (2007). Pharmacology for Health Professionals (2nd ed.). Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

Drug classes, mechanisms of action, clinical applications.

MIMS Australia. (2025). MIMS Online. https://www.mims.com.au/

Current Australian drug database. Always check here for up-to-date prescribing info.

Khoo, J.-P. (2012). Mood stabilisers. Australian Prescriber, 35(5), 164-168. https://doi.org/10.18773/austprescr.2012.070

Lithium, valproate, carbamazepine. Narrow therapeutic ranges, monitoring requirements. Key for bipolar pharmacology.

\uD83E\uDDE0 Mental Health & Psychiatric Nursing

Foster, K., Marks, P., O'Brien, A., & Raeburn, T. (Eds.). (2021). Mental Health in Nursing: Theory and Practice for Clinical Settings (5th ed.). Elsevier.

Core MH nursing text. Assessment, therapeutic relationships, interventions, specific disorders.

Meadows, G., Farhall, J., Fossey, E., Grigg, M., McDermott, F., & Singh, B. S. (2012). Mental Health in Australia: Collaborative Community Practice. Oxford University Press.

Australian MH context, assessment frameworks, service delivery, cultural considerations, community MH.

American Psychiatric Association. (2013). Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (5th ed.). APA Publishing.

DSM-5: The diagnostic bible. Criteria for bipolar, schizophrenia, depression, anxiety, and all psychiatric conditions.

Swartz, H. A., & Suppes, T. (Eds.). (2019). Bipolar II Disorder: Recognition, Understanding, and Treatment. APA Publishing.

Bipolar disorder deep-dive. Treatment modalities, psychosocial interventions, medication management.

Mitchell, P. (2012). Bipolar disorder. Australian Family Physician, 42(9). https://www.racgp.org.au/

Australian GP perspective on bipolar. Good for understanding primary care referral pathways.

Volcevska, E., Luck, L., Elmir, R., Dickens, O. L., & Murphy, G. (2023). Nurses' experiences when conducting the mental state examination (RMSE): A scoping review. International Journal of Mental Health Nursing, 32(2), 224-240.

How nurses actually experience doing MSEs. Practical challenges, skills required. Excellent for MSE assessment discussions.

Weiner, L., Doignon-Camus, N., Bertschy, G., & Giersch, A. (2019). Thought and language disturbance in bipolar disorder. Scientific Reports, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-50818-5

Pressure of speech, flight of ideas, racing thoughts in mania. Key for MSE discussions.

NSW Health. (2022). Mental Health Outcomes and Assessment Tool (MH-OAT). https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/

NSW standardised MH assessment tool. Relevant for NSW-based placements and practice.

Gaskin, C., & Gavin, D. (2018). Recognising Signs of Deterioration in a Person's Mental State. ACSQHC. https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/

Early warning signs of MH deterioration. Links to NSQHS Standards. Assessment and escalation.

Semahegn, A., Torpey, K., Manu, A., et al. (2020). Psychotropic medication non-adherence and its associated factors among patients with major psychiatric disorders. Systematic Reviews, 9(1), 1-18.

Why patients stop taking psych meds. Factors, consequences, nursing strategies to improve adherence.

Shah, N., Grover, S., & Rao, G. (2019). Clinical practice guidelines for management of bipolar disorder. Indian Journal of Psychiatry, 59(5), 51.

International bipolar management guidelines. Pharmacological and psychosocial approaches.

\uD83E\uDDD8 Mindfulness in Healthcare

Kabat-Zinn, J. (2003). Mindfulness-based intervention in context: Past, present and future. Clinical Psychology: Science and Practice, 10(2), 144-156.

The foundational paper on MBSR. Kabat-Zinn's definition of mindfulness used in every nursing MH text.

Speca, M., Carlson, L. E., Goodey, E., et al. (2006). A randomized, wait-list controlled clinical trial: The effect of a mindfulness meditation-based stress reduction program. Psychosomatic Medicine, 62, 613-622.

Evidence for mindfulness in cancer patient stress reduction. Good RCT example for EBP discussions.

Pipe, T. B., Bortz, J. J., Dueck, A., et al. (2009). Nurse leader mindfulness meditation program: A randomized controlled trial. Journal of Nursing Administration, 39(3), 130-137.

Mindfulness for nurses themselves. Stress management, burnout prevention.

Germer, C. K., Siegel, R. D., & Fulton, P. R. (2005). Mindfulness and Psychotherapy. New York: Guilford Press.

Integration of mindfulness into therapeutic practice. Broader psychotherapy context.

\u2764\uFE0F Cardiac & Emergency

Curtis, K., Ramsden, C., & Friendship, J. (2007). Emergency & Trauma Nursing. Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

Emergency assessment (ABC, GCS), stroke care, trauma management, triage.

Elliot, D., Aitken, L., & Chaboyer, W. (2007). ACCCN's Critical Care Nursing. Marrickville: Elsevier Australia.

ICU nursing, hemodynamic monitoring, ventilation, critical care pharmacology.

Porth, C. (2005). Pathophysiology: Concepts of Altered Health States (7th ed.). Philadelphia: Lippincott Williams & Wilkins.

Deep pathophysiology. Cardiac, respiratory, neuro, renal.

Collins, C. (2007). Pathophysiology and classification of stroke. Nursing Standard, 21(28), 35-39.

Stroke types (ischaemic vs haemorrhagic), risk factors, classification systems.

Cross, S., & Walker, C. (2008). Stroke care: A nursing perspective. Nursing Standard, 22(23), 47.

Nursing-specific stroke care. Assessment, monitoring, rehabilitation, patient education.

\uD83D\uDC76 Paediatrics & Respiratory

Children's Health Queensland Hospital and Health Service. (2022). Queensland Paediatric Emergency Care Nursing Skill Sheets.

How children are anatomically/physiologically different. Essential for paediatric respiratory assessment.

National Asthma Council [NAC]. (2025). Asthma. https://www.nationalasthma.org.au/

Australian asthma guidelines, action plans, medication info. The authoritative source.

Royal Children's Hospital Melbourne [RCHM]. (2023). Clinical Practice Guidelines.

Paediatric clinical guidelines: asthma, respiratory, medication dosing. Free online.

\uD83D\uDCCB Clinical Standards & Guidelines

Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care [ACSQHC]. (2021). National Safety and Quality Health Service Standards (2nd ed.).

The 8 national standards. Comprehensive care, medication safety, clinical governance.

Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia [NMBA]. (2016). Registered Nurse Standards for Practice.

Scope of practice, professional conduct, clinical competence expectations.

Christman, E., & Ernstmeyer, K. (2022). Nursing: Mental Health and Community Concepts. Chippewa Valley Technical College.

Open-access nursing textbook. Mental health, community nursing concepts. Free resource.

\uD83D\uDD0D How to Use This Library

1. Start with your unit guide's required readings. 2. Use this list to find additional sources. 3. Always check publication dates for newer editions. 4. Cross-reference with UNE Library databases (CINAHL, Medline, PubMed). 5. These are scaffolding, not substitutes for your own research.
