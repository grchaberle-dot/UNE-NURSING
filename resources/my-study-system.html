\uD83D\uDD04 The Study Loop

Every topic follows this cycle:

  1. \uD83D\uDCD6 Learn: Read through content cards with key terms highlighted and tips
  2. \uD83C\uDFAF Practice: Low-stakes matching, drag-and-drop, ordering exercises
  3. \uD83D\uDCDD Test: Timed quiz, scored, wrong answers flagged with explanations
  4. \u274C Review: See exactly what you got wrong and why
  5. \uD83D\uDD04 Retry: Re-quiz ONLY the items you failed until you pass them all
This is spaced repetition + active recall. The gold standard for health sciences. 80% to pass a module, then revisit it a week later.

\uD83D\uDCDA After Every Session/Lecture/Workshop

  • Auto-create session summary (detailed, Speechify-length for audio)
  • Create tasks for follow-up items
  • Build interactive flashcards, quiz, and diagrams
  • Push to BOTH the ClickUp artifact (interactive practice) AND GitHub (full reference)

\uD83E\uDDE0 Nursing-Specific Study Methods

Interactive anatomy

Drag-and-drop labeling for body parts, heart chambers, organs, brain structures. Visual learning that beats reading a textbook.

Clinical scenario decision trees

"Patient presents with..." branching scenarios. Choose assessments, interventions, escalation paths. Wrong choices explain why.

Drug calculation engine

Randomised dosage problems with IV drip rates, unit conversions, weight-based dosing. Timed mode for exam prep. Dosage calc exams are pass/fail!

Spaced repetition flashcards

Confidence-rated cards that come back more often if you're unsure. Cover every topic from abbreviations to disease processes.
