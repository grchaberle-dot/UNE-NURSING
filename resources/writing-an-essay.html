📝 Part 1: Prep (Steps 1–7)

  1. Choose the question: think about time available, reference material, and your interests
  2. Analyse the question: find the instruction words, topic words and restricting words/phrases
  3. Rewrite the question in your own words
  4. Brainstorm what you already know. Nothing coming? Do some preliminary reading
  5. Research: examples, evidence, quotes. Record your sources accurately AS YOU GO
  6. Decide the order: decreasing importance is a common one
  7. Plan: about one page max

The Essay Plan

  • Question: write it out
  • Intro: what you understand the question to mean, your direction and your angle. Agree or disagree, and why?
  • Body: one main point per paragraph plus evidence, ordered most to least important
  • Conclusion: tentative for now, a general comment on your points

✏️ Part 2: Writing (Steps 8–12)

Intro

Identify the topic, give essential background and key definitions, then show your direction (outline of the body topics) and your angle (thesis statement).

Body

Main points plus evidence. Good plan = turning notes into full sentences.

Conclusion

Summarise the main points, make a judgement, and link back to the question.

  1. First draft: write it out in full
  2. Revise (checklist below) + spelling, sentence structure, punctuation
  3. Second draft: meets all requirements, including references
  4. Print and proofread on paper
  5. Re-read OUT LOUD, and get someone else to read it too

Step 9 Revision Checklist

  • Does the intro identify the topic?
  • Are there enough points?
  • Does each point have its own paragraph?
  • Does each paragraph have a clear topic sentence?
  • Is there enough supporting evidence in each paragraph?
  • Are the paragraphs in the best order?
  • Does each paragraph flow smoothly into the next?
  • Does the conclusion summarise or comment on the main points?
  • Have I answered the question?
