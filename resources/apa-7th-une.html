🔑 Core Rules

  • In-text = 2–3 parts: surname(s), year, + page/para for direct quotes
  • Reference = 4 parts: author, date, title, source (+ link)
  • References page: new page, References bold + centred, 1.5 or double spacing, no extra gaps, hanging indent
  • Sentence case = first word + proper nouns. Title case = all words of 4+ letters
20 marks for APA in EVERY HLTH101 assessment = 60 marks total. Worth nailing.

🔗 Links

  • DOI = https://doi.org/10.xxxx. Always use it if there is one. No full stop after it
  • From a library database (CINAHL, MEDLINE, ProQuest…) or myLearn with no DOI = reference it like print, no URL
  • Never put a URL in-text
  • Pages that change = n.d. + "Retrieved Month Day, Year, from URL"

Book (edition)

Heffernan, J. A. W., Lincoln, E. J., & Atwill, J. (2001). Writing: A college handbook (5th ed.). W.W. Norton.

Chapter in an edited book

Phillips, J. (1999). The psychodynamic narrative. In G. Roberts & J. Holmes (Eds.), Healing stories: Narrative in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2nd ed., pp. 7–15). Oxford University Press.

News website (👉 Dalton, 2026)

Webpage, group author

ABS (👉 HLTH101 Module 6)

Australian Bureau of Statistics. (2006). Alcohol consumption in Australia: A snapshot, 2004-05 (No. 4832.0.55.001). http://www.abs.gov.au

In-text the first time: (Australian Bureau of Statistics [ABS], 2006), then (ABS, 2006) after that.

HLTH101 myLearn content (unit's own format)

Robertson, S. (2026). Module and Topic number and title of the page in HLTH101: Information and Evidence in Health and Social Care [Unit Notes]. School of Health, University of New England.

📊 Tables & Figures

  • Mention it in your paragraph, then place it directly below that paragraph
  • Above it: the number in bold (Figure 1), then the caption on the next line in italic title case
  • Below it: a Note. with the source and copyright. It still needs a reference entry
