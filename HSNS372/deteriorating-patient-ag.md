# Deteriorating Patient & A-G Assessment

## A-G Framework
- A (Airway): patency, stridor, wheeze, snoring
- B (Breathing): WOB, resp rate, SpO2
- C (Circulation): perfusion, pulse, BP
- D (Disability): AVPU, GCS, PEARL
- E (Examination/Exposure): visual/physical inspection
- F (Fluids): I&O, hydration
- G (Glucose): BGL, electrolytes

## FAST Stroke Assessment
Facial weakness, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to act fast

## Stroke Types
- Ischaemic: embolic (clot from heart) or thrombotic (plaque in artery)
- Haemorrhagic: intracerebral or subarachnoid
- TIA: temporary "mini stroke"

## Sepsis (3 Rs)
1. Recognise: risk factors, signs
2. Resuscitate: IV fluids 30mL/kg, IVAB within 1hr, 2 blood cultures
3. Refer: senior clinicians, ISBAR
