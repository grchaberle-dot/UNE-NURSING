# BLS & CPR

## DRSABCD
Danger → Response → Send for help → Airway → Breathing → CPR → Defibrillation

## Adult CPR
- Rate: 100-120/min
- Depth: 5-6cm
- Hands: centre of chest, lower half of sternum
- 30:2 ratio (manual), 1 breath every 6 sec (intubated)
- Pause every 2 min (max 10 sec) to assess pulse

## AED
Upper right chest + left lateral below nipple. Call CLEAR, shock, resume compressions immediately.

## Defibrillation vs Cardioversion
- Defib: unsynchronised, VF/pulseless VT, no sedation
- Cardioversion: synchronised, unstable tachy WITH pulse

## Chain of Survival
Early recognition (000) → Early CPR → Early defib → Early advanced care → Post-resus care
