# Diabetes Emergencies: DKA, HHS & Hypoglycaemia

## DKA
Insulin deficiency → fat breakdown → ketones → metabolic acidosis + osmotic diuresis → dehydration
Signs: Kussmaul breathing, fruity breath, flushed dry skin
Management: insulin, IV fluids, monitor electrolytes/cardiac, treat trigger

## HHS
Enough insulin to prevent ketones but NOT hyperglycaemia. Very high BGL, severe dehydration, altered mental state. Neuro signs may mimic stroke → always check BGL

## DKA vs HHS
- DKA: ketones present, acidosis, Kussmaul breathing, usually T1DM
- HHS: no/minimal ketones, very high osmolality, usually T2DM

## Hypoglycaemia
Too little glucose for brain. Signs: confusion, trembling, sweating, seizures
Management: check BGL → fast-acting glucose (rule of fifteen) → recheck → identify cause

## Complications
- Macrovascular: stroke, MI, peripheral vascular disease
- Microvascular: retinopathy, neuropathy, nephropathy (appear 10-20yr post diagnosis)
