# Clinical Governance, Safety & Quality

## Definition
Clinical governance is the integrated system of leadership, culture, processes and structures for safe, effective, accountable and person-centred healthcare through continuous improvement.

## National Model (5 Elements)
1. Leadership and culture
2. Safety and quality systems
3. Clinical performance and effectiveness
4. Environment of care
5. Partnering with consumers

## NSQHS 8 Standards
1. Clinical Governance
2. Partnering with Consumers
3. Preventing and Controlling Infections
4. Medication Safety
5. Comprehensive Care
6. Communicating for Safety
7. Blood Management
8. Recognising and Responding to Acute Deterioration

## Just Culture
Supports reporting while distinguishing human error from risky behaviour from intentional misconduct. Blame culture reduces reporting and learning.

## Open Disclosure
Honest discussion with patient/family about incidents that caused harm.
