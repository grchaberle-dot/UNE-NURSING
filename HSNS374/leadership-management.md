# Nursing Leadership & Management

## Leadership vs Management
- Leadership: influences people toward shared goals, vision, advocacy, any nurse can demonstrate
- Management: coordinates operations, resources, planning, formal responsibility

## Leadership Theories
- Transformational, Transactional, Servant, Authentic

## Scope of Practice
Full range of roles a nurse is educated, authorised and competent to perform. NMBA Decision-Making Framework guides decisions.

## Delegation
Only delegate within the other person's competence and scope. Provide clear instructions. Follow up. Remain accountable.
