📝 Document at every step.
A

Assessment

Collect and process data about the client.

  • Collect data from all sources
  • Identify problems
  • Analyse and interpret data
Assessment always comes first. "What should the nurse do first?" = usually Assess.
D

Diagnosis

Identify nursing diagnoses. NOT a medical diagnosis.

  • Impaired skin integrity
  • Risk for infection
  • Pain
  • Ineffective airway clearance
P

Planning

Formulate steps to achieve desired outcomes.

  • Prioritise needs
  • Establish goals
  • Construct action plan
  • Devise evaluation parameters
I

Implementation

Translate the care plan into action.

  • Put nursing plan into action
  • Coordinate care
  • Delegate and supervise
  • Provide health education
  • Document responses
If you didn't document it, it didn't happen!
E

Evaluation

Judge whether objectives were met.

  • Assess care outcomes
  • Identify needed alterations
  • Revise plans as necessary

🧑‍⚕️ Roles of the Community Health Nurse

  • Clinician: Health in community context
  • Advocate: Self-care and self-determination
  • Collaborator: Strengths toward common goals
  • Researcher: Data to predict and modify
  • Counselor: Listening and feedback
  • Case Manager: Coordinate cost-efficient care
  • Educator: Knowledge for appropriate choices
  • Hospice Care: Balancing client needs
