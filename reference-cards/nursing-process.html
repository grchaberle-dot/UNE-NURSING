\uD83D\uDCCB Nursing Process (ADPIE)

The 5-Step Framework for Patient Care

\u2190 Back to Hub
A

Assessment

Collect and process data about the client. This is the foundation.

  • Collect data from all possible sources (patient, family, records, observation)
  • Identify problems
  • Analyse and interpret data
Assessment always comes first. You can't diagnose, plan, or treat without it. "What should the nurse do first?" = usually Assess.
D

Diagnosis

Identify nursing diagnoses based on assessment data. This is NOT a medical diagnosis.

  • Impaired skin integrity
  • Risk for infection
  • Pain
  • Ineffective airway clearance
  • Body image disturbance
P

Planning

Formulate the steps to achieve desired outcomes.

  • Prioritise needs
  • Establish goals based on needs and capabilities
  • Construct action and operation plan
  • Devise evaluation parameters
  • Revise plan as needs change
I

Implementation

Translate the care plan into action.

  • Initiate contact, demonstrate caring attributes
  • Develop mutual trust
  • Put nursing plan into action
  • Coordinate care and services
  • Utilise community resources
  • Delegate and supervise
  • Monitor health services provided
  • Provide health education
  • Document responses to nursing actions
If you didn't document it, it didn't happen! Documentation is a critical part of implementation.
E

Evaluation

Judge whether objectives were met.

  • Assess care outcomes
  • Performance appraisal
  • Estimate cost-benefit ratio
  • Identify needed alterations
  • Revise plans as necessary

\uD83E\uDDD1\u200D\u2695\uFE0F Roles of the Community Health Nurse

  • Clinician: Focus on health in community context
  • Advocate: Promote self-care and self-determination
  • Collaborator: Bring together strengths toward common goals
  • Researcher: Utilise data to predict and modify interventions
  • Counselor: Listening, feedback, and information
  • Case Manager: Oversee all aspects, coordinate cost-efficient care
  • Educator: Provide knowledge for appropriate choices
  • Hospice Care: Balancing client needs in home/other settings
