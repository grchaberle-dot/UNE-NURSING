\u2728 What is Improvement Science?

A methodology for addressing identified problems in healthcare:

  1. Identify a problem
  2. Define the problem clearly
  3. Diagnose the causes
  4. Develop change ideas (possible solutions)
  5. Test and implement interventions via PDSA cycles

\uD83D\uDD04 The Four Stages

P

Plan

Define the test and make a prediction.

  • What is the objective of the test?
  • What change idea will be tested?
  • What do you predict will happen?
  • Who, where, when?
  • What data will be collected, how, and by whom?
  • What tasks must be done beforehand?
D

Do

Carry out the test and collect data.

  • Document measurable findings
  • Record anecdotes and feedback from staff and consumers
S

Study

Compare findings with predictions. Ask:

  • Has the test resulted in an improvement?
  • If not, what exactly needs to change?
  • Can it be tested on a larger scale?
  • Does it need adjusting or different conditions?
  • What was learned?
A

Act

Respond to results and plan the next cycle.

  • Continue the change
  • Modify and retest
  • Test under different conditions
  • Increase the scale

\uD83D\uDCC8 Small Scale \u2192 Scale Up

Start tiny, then grow:

1 patient \u2192 3 patients \u2192 7 patients \u2192 all relevant patients

Each cycle learns, adjusts, and expands. You never go big first.

PDSA is cyclical. Each "Act" stage feeds into the next "Plan." You keep cycling until the improvement is validated at full scale.
