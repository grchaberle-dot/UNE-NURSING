✨ What is Improvement Science?

A methodology for addressing identified problems in healthcare:

  1. Identify a problem
  2. Define the problem clearly
  3. Diagnose the causes
  4. Develop change ideas
  5. Test and implement via PDSA cycles

🔄 The Four Stages

💡 Small tests of change = big improvements

PDSA is a learning loop: each Act informs the next Plan.

Predict, Do, Scrutinise, Adapt: "Please Do Study Again" keeps improvement moving.
🔎 Study ≠ just check, it means ANALYSE

🟢 Plan

  • What is the objective of the test?
  • What change idea will be tested?
  • What do you predict will happen?
  • Who, where, when?
  • What data will be collected?

🟡 Do

  • Carry out the test and collect data
  • Document measurable findings
  • Record anecdotes and feedback

🟣 Study

  • Compare findings with predictions
  • Has the test resulted in improvement?
  • Can it be tested on a larger scale?
  • What was learned?

🌸 Act

  • Continue the change
  • Modify and retest
  • Test under different conditions
  • Increase the scale

📈 Small Scale → Scale Up

1 patient → 3 patients → 7 patients → all relevant patients

Each cycle learns, adjusts, and expands. You never go big first.

PDSA is cyclical. Each "Act" stage feeds into the next "Plan." You keep cycling until the improvement is validated at full scale.
