📈 Normal Sinus Rhythm

💓 Rate 60–100 = NSR

📏 Wide QRS = think ventricular origin

What to look for

Regular rhythm, rate 60–100, one upright P before each QRS, constant PR interval, narrow QRS.

〰️ Common Arrhythmias

AF vs Normal

AF = "irregularly irregular": R–R intervals vary unpredictably

AF = IRREGULARLY IRREGULAR
No consistent P waves. Ventricular response may be fast, slow, or variable.

VF = Shockable Chaos

Disorganised ventricular activity, no cardiac output. Activate emergency response immediately.

ECG interpretation = pattern recognition + patient assessment. Concerning rhythm + instability = emergency.
