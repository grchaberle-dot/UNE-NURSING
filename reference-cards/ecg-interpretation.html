\uD83D\uDCCF ECG Strip Basics

Strip Boxes

  • 1 small box = 0.04 seconds
  • 1 large box = 0.20 seconds
  • 5 large boxes = 1 second

Normal Intervals

  • PR Interval: 0.12 - 0.20 seconds
  • QRS Complex: 0.06 - 0.12 seconds
  • QT Interval: <0.44s (male), <0.46s (female)

Basic Rhythm Rates

  • Normal Sinus Rhythm: 60 - 100 bpm
  • Tachycardia: >100 bpm
  • Bradycardia: <60 bpm

\uD83D\uDD12 Heart Blocks

TypeKey FeatureMemory Trick
1st Degree AV BlockProlonged PR interval (>0.20s), all P-waves conducted"If the R is far from the P, then you have FIRST DEGREE"
2nd Degree Type I (Wenckebach)PR interval progressively lengthens until a QRS is dropped"Longer, longer, longer, DROP... then you have a WENCKEBACH"
2nd Degree Type II (Mobitz II)Some P-waves not conducted (dropped QRS), PR interval constant"If some P's don't get through, prepare to pace that MOBITZ II"
3rd Degree (Complete)No relationship between P-waves and QRS, atria and ventricles beat independently"If P's and Q's don't agree, then you have a THIRD DEGREE"

\uD83D\uDCDD STEMI vs NSTEMI

STEMI

  • ST Elevation Myocardial Infarction
  • Complete coronary artery blockage
  • ST segment is ELEVATED
  • Biomarkers (troponin): elevated
  • Treatment: emergency cath lab

NSTEMI

  • Non ST Elevation Myocardial Infarction
  • Partial (incomplete) blockage
  • ST segment is NOT elevated (ST depression or T inversion instead)
  • Biomarkers (troponin): elevated
  • Treatment: medical management first

\u2705 High-Yield Fill-in-the-Blank

  1. The Sinoatrial (SA) node is the primary pacemaker of the heart
  2. Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT) is an extremely rapid rhythm originating above the ventricle
  3. The cardiac biomarker Troponin (I or T) is the most specific and sensitive indicator of myocardial infarction
  4. Synchronized cardioversion can be used for stable or unstable cardiac rhythms
  5. A therapeutic INR for warfarin in A-Fib is between 2.0 and 3.0
  6. Beck's triad (cardiac tamponade): muffled heart sounds, hypotension, and jugular vein distension
  7. Before administering digoxin, check apical pulse x1 min, hold if HR below 60 bpm
  8. PEA stands for Pulseless Electrical Activity
  9. Peaked T-waves are associated with elevated serum potassium
