🔁 Cardiac Cycle

DIASTOLErelax + fill
→
SYSTOLEcontract + eject
→
DIASTOLErepeat

Diastole: ventricles relax and fill. Systole: ventricles contract and eject blood.

S1 = LUB • S2 = DUB
S1: AV valves (mitral + tricuspid) shut at start of systole
S2: semilunar valves (aortic + pulmonary) shut at start of diastole
