📋 Each Step in Detail

✅ 3 identifiers:
Name, DOB, UR number

Always confirm who you're speaking to

I – Identify

Your name, role, location. Patient's full name, DOB, UR number. Confirm who you're speaking with.

S – Situation

What is happening RIGHT NOW. State your concern clearly. Current vital signs. Any acute change from baseline.

Lead with the concern, not the history. "I'm worried about Mrs Smith's breathing" not "Mrs Smith was admitted three days ago with..."
🚨 Don't bury the concern!

"I'm calling because..." should come EARLY

B – Background

Reason for admission, diagnosis, relevant history, current meds and allergies, recent investigations.

A – Assessment

Your clinical impression. Relevant trends. Are MET criteria met or approaching? What have you already done?

R – Recommendation

What you need to happen. Who should review. When. What should change. Be specific: "I need a medical review within 30 minutes."

Read back: confirm the plan, document the conversation, and follow up if the response doesn't come.
