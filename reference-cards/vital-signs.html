\u2764\uFE0F Adult Vital Signs

Vital SignNormal RangeKey Notes
Temperature36.1 - 37.2\u00B0C (oral)Rectal is ~0.5\u00B0C higher, axillary ~0.5\u00B0C lower
Heart Rate (Pulse)60 - 100 bpmBradycardia <60, Tachycardia >100
Respiratory Rate12 - 20 breaths/minCount for full 60 seconds. Tachypnea >20, Bradypnea <12
Blood PressureSystolic: 90-120 mmHg
Diastolic: 60-80 mmHg		Hypertension: >140/90. Hypotension: <90/60
Oxygen Saturation95 - 100%<90% = hypoxemia, needs intervention
Pain0 (no pain)0-10 scale. Subjective: "pain is whatever the patient says it is"
Vital signs are assessed together, not in isolation. A patient with HR 110 AND BP 85/50 AND RR 24 is showing signs of shock. Pattern recognition is more important than memorising individual numbers.
