📊 Normal Adult Vital Signs

⚠️ RR is the FIRST vital sign to change in deterioration!

Count for a full 60 seconds. Don't estimate.

Memorise these. You'll use them every single shift.

🚨 Red Flags

📞 Report MAP < 65

MAP = (2×diastolic + systolic) ÷ 3

Below 65 = organs not perfusing
VitalWorry When...
🌡️ Temp< 36°C or > 38.5°C
❤️ HR< 50 or > 120 bpm
🫁 RR< 10 or > 25 /min
🩸 BPSystolic < 90 or > 180
💨 SpO2< 92% (or < 88% in COPD)
Any single abnormal vital sign warrants reassessment. Two or more = escalate using ISBAR.
RR IS THE CANARY
Respiratory rate is the canary in the coal mine: it's often the first vital sign to change when a patient is deteriorating. Count it properly!
