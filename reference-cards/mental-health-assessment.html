⚠️ Safety First

Always assess safety and risk of harm to self or others FIRST. Suicidal ideation, homicidal ideation, acute psychosis, and severe agitation require immediate safety assessment and intervention.

🧠 Mental State Examination (MSE)

👁️ Safety first: always assess risk before everything else.

The 8 domains of the Mental State Examination.

A B S MATT P C I
Appearance • Behaviour • Speech • Mood/Affect • Thought • Perception • Cognition • Insight/Judgement

📋 MSE Domain Details

👀 Observe before you ask: appearance and behaviour tell you a lot before the interview starts.

Appearance

  • Grooming, hygiene, clothing
  • Age-appropriate? Well-nourished?
  • Eye contact, posture, body language
  • Visible injuries, self-harm marks

Behaviour

  • Psychomotor agitation or retardation
  • Cooperative, guarded, hostile, withdrawn
  • Unusual movements, tics, tremors

Speech

  • Rate, rhythm, volume, tone
  • Pressured, slowed, monotone
  • Coherent? Spontaneous or only when prompted?

Mood & Affect

  • Mood: Patient's subjective description ("How are you feeling?")
  • Affect: What YOU observe (flat, blunted, labile, congruent with mood)
  • Range, reactivity, congruence with content

Thought

  • Form: Logical? Tangential? Flight of ideas? Loosening of associations?
  • Content: Delusions, obsessions, phobias, suicidal/homicidal ideation
  • Thought blocking, broadcasting, insertion, withdrawal

Perception

  • Hallucinations (auditory, visual, tactile, olfactory)
  • Illusions, depersonalisation, derealisation

Cognition

  • Orientation (time, place, person)
  • Attention, concentration, memory
  • MMSE or similar screening tool

Insight & Judgement

  • Does the person recognise they are unwell?
  • Do they understand the need for treatment?
  • Can they make safe, reasonable decisions?

🗣️ Therapeutic Communication

  • Active listening: Full attention, minimal interruption
  • Open-ended questions: "Tell me about..." not "Are you sad?"
  • Reflection: "It sounds like you're feeling overwhelmed"
  • Silence: Allow space for processing
  • Summarising: Check understanding
  • Avoid: Giving advice, false reassurance, minimising, being judgemental

🚨 Risk Assessment

Assess for:

  • Suicidal ideation, plan, means, intent, previous attempts
  • Self-harm (current and history)
  • Risk to others (homicidal ideation, history of violence)
  • Vulnerability (exploitation, neglect, homelessness)
  • Substance use and its impact on risk
Ask directly about suicide. It does NOT increase risk. "Are you having thoughts of ending your life?" is a safe and necessary question.
