🇦🇺 NMBA & Registration

🛡️ AHPRA = your registration. Guard it.
  • AHPRA (Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency): administers registration
  • NMBA (Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia): sets standards, codes, and guidelines
  • Registration required to practise as an EN, RN, or NP in Australia
  • Annual renewal with CPD requirements
  • Mandatory notifications for certain conduct or health issues

📋 NMBA Standards for Practice

💡 Standards = what you MUST meet. Codes = how you should BEHAVE.

The 7 NMBA Standards for Practice: know them, live them, document them.

⚖️ Scope of Practice

📋 Scope = what you're trained AND authorised to do.

Scope of practice is determined by:

  • Education, training, and competence
  • Legislation and regulation
  • Employer policies and position description
  • Professional standards and codes
  • Context of practice (setting, patient population)
"Am I educated, authorised, and competent?" If all three = yes, it's within your scope. If any = no, escalate or refer.

EN → RN → NP: increasing education, scope, and autonomy.

📜 Codes and Conduct

📝 Code of Conduct = minimum behaviour standard. Breach = AHPRA notification.

Code of Conduct

  • Legal and ethical practice
  • Person-centred practice
  • Cultural practice and respectful relationships
  • Professional behaviour
  • Teaching, supervising, and assessing
  • Research and evidence-based practice
  • Health and wellbeing of self

Code of Ethics

  • Values: respect, justice, beneficence, non-maleficence
  • Ethical principles guide decision-making
  • Patient autonomy and informed consent
  • Confidentiality and privacy
  • Advocacy for vulnerable populations
  • Truthfulness and integrity
BEN JAR
Beneficence (do good) • Ethics • Non-maleficence (do no harm) • Justice (fairness) • Autonomy (patient choice) • Respect

🛡️ Mandatory Notifications

⚠️ You are legally obligated to report. It's not optional.

Health practitioners, employers, and education providers must notify AHPRA if they reasonably believe a practitioner has:

  • Practised while intoxicated by alcohol or drugs
  • Engaged in sexual misconduct with a patient
  • Placed the public at substantial risk of harm due to an impairment
  • Placed the public at substantial risk due to a significant departure from accepted professional standards
Mandatory notification is a legal obligation under the National Law. Failing to report when required is itself a breach.

🌏 Cultural Safety

  • Cultural safety goes beyond awareness or sensitivity
  • The patient/family defines whether care is culturally safe
  • Acknowledge power imbalances
  • Respect Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples' connection to Country, family, and community
  • Ask: "Is there anything about your culture or beliefs that would help me care for you better?"
  • Closing the Gap: address health inequity for First Nations peoples

📚 Continuing Professional Development

  • Minimum 20 hours CPD per year (NMBA requirement)
  • Must include at least one activity relating to professional practice
  • Keep a CPD portfolio documenting activities and reflections
  • Self-assessment against NMBA standards at renewal
  • May be audited: maintain records for 5 years
