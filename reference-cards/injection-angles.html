Angle and depth: ID → SC → IM

10-15°

Intradermal (ID)

  • Location: Inner lower arm, upper chest/back
  • Uses: Allergy testing, TB/Mantoux testing
  • Gauge: 25, 26, or 27
  • Technique: Bevel UP, inject slowly to form wheal
  • Key rule: Do NOT massage the site
🫧 Tiny wheal = right layer.
45°

Subcutaneous (SC)

  • Uses: Vaccines, heparin, insulin, narcotics
  • Key rules: Small doses only, rotate sites
🧈 Pinch the fat, control the angle.
90°

Intramuscular (IM)

  • Needle: 1” to 1.5”
  • Sites: Deltoid, vastus lateralis, ventrogluteal
  • Technique: Clean with alcohol, inject slowly
⚠️ Z-track for irritating meds.

Intravenous (IV)

  • Fastest route, immediate onset
  • Cannot be retrieved once given
📋 Aspirate debate: check facility policy.
ID nearly flat (10-15°), SC halfway (45°), IM straight down (90°). Deeper tissue = steeper angle.
