Head-to-toe assessment regions

TOP TO BOTTOM, OUTSIDE TO INSIDE
Start at the head, work down systematically. Inspect before you palpate. Compare bilaterally.

🧠 General Survey & Head

👀 Always compare bilaterally!

General Survey

  • Overall appearance, hygiene, grooming
  • Level of consciousness, orientation
  • Body habitus, posture, gait
  • Signs of distress, pain, anxiety
  • Vital signs: T, HR, BP, RR, SpO2

Head & Face

  • Inspect: symmetry, skin colour, lesions, oedema
  • Palpate: scalp, temporal arteries, sinuses, TMJ
  • Cranial nerve assessment (as indicated)

Eyes

  • Vision acuity, visual fields
  • Pupil size, shape, reactivity (PERRLA)
  • Conjunctivae (pale = anaemia), sclera (yellow = jaundice)
  • Eye movement: CN III, IV, VI

Ears, Nose, Mouth

  • Ears: hearing, otoscopic exam, discharge
  • Nose: patency, discharge, septum deviation
  • Mouth: mucous membranes, tongue, dentition, gag reflex

🫁 Neck & Chest

💡 Inspect BEFORE palpate.

Neck

  • Inspect: symmetry, JVP, thyroid
  • Palpate: lymph nodes, thyroid, trachea (midline)
  • Range of motion

Respiratory

  • Inspect: chest shape, symmetry, respiratory effort, use of accessory muscles
  • Palpate: chest expansion, tactile fremitus
  • Percuss: resonance (normal), dullness (fluid/mass), hyperresonance (pneumothorax)
  • Auscultate: breath sounds bilaterally, adventitious sounds (crackles, wheezes, stridor)

Cardiovascular

  • Inspect: JVP, precordial heave
  • Palpate: PMI (apex beat), thrills
  • Auscultate: S1, S2, murmurs, extra heart sounds (S3, S4)
  • Peripheral: pulses, capillary refill, oedema, skin colour/temp

🦴 Abdomen & Extremities

Abdomen

  • Inspect: contour, symmetry, scars, distension
  • Auscultate FIRST: bowel sounds (before palpation)
  • Percuss: tympany (normal), dullness (organ borders)
  • Palpate: tenderness, masses, organomegaly (light then deep)
Abdomen: auscultate BEFORE palpate or percuss. Touching the abdomen first can alter bowel sounds.

Musculoskeletal

  • Inspect: deformity, swelling, muscle wasting
  • Range of motion (active then passive)
  • Muscle strength (0-5 scale, bilaterally)
  • Gait assessment

Neurological

  • GCS, orientation, cognition
  • Cranial nerves (as indicated)
  • Sensation: light touch, pain, temperature
  • Reflexes: deep tendon (0-4 scale)
  • Coordination: finger-to-nose, heel-to-shin
  • Balance: Romberg test

📝 Skin Assessment

  • Colour, temperature, moisture, turgor
  • Lesions: type, size, shape, colour, distribution
  • Pressure areas: sacrum, heels, occiput, elbows
  • Wounds: location, size, stage, exudate, odour
  • IV sites: phlebitis signs, patency
