⚠️ If it isn't documented, legally it didn't happen.

⚖️ Why Documentation Matters

  • Legal document: subpoenaed in court, AHPRA investigations
  • Continuity of care: next nurse needs your notes
  • NSQHS Standard 6: accurate, timely, accessible records
  • Clinical safety: omissions = adverse events

🇦🇺 Golden Rules

  • Black pen only
  • Date and time every entry
  • Sign with full name and designation
  • Never erase: single line through errors
  • Never document in advance
  • Be specific and objective

📋 The CHIMNEOPS Domains

Scan each relevant domain, then document objectively.

CHIMNEOPS
Clever Healthy Individuals Must Never Ever Overlook Patient Safety
Cognitive · Hygiene · Integrity · Mobility · Nutrition · Endocrine · Elimination · Observation · Psychosocial · Safety

C — Cognitive

Orientation, GCS, confusion, anxiety

e.g. Alert and oriented x3. GCS 15/15.

H — Hygiene

Self-care, wash, oral care, grooming

e.g. Hygiene independent. Skin clean and dry.

I — Integrity (Skin)

Wounds, pressure areas, IV sites, oedema

e.g. Skin intact. IV cannula R) forearm patent.

M — Mobility

Ambulation, falls risk, aids

e.g. Mobilised with standby assist x1. Falls risk high.

N — Nutrition

Oral intake, IV fluids, diet, nausea

e.g. Tolerated full diet. Fluids encouraged.

E — Endocrine

BGL, diabetes, hormonal

e.g. BGL 6.2 mmol/L pre-meal. Insulin given.

E — Elimination

Urine, bowel, catheter, continence

e.g. Voided x2. Clear yellow urine.

O — Observation

Vitals: T, HR, BP, RR, SpO2, pain

e.g. T 36.8, HR 78, BP 128/76, RR 16, SpO2 98% RA.

P — Psychosocial

Mood, anxiety, family, supports

e.g. Anxious re: procedure. Family supportive.

S — Safety

Pain, positioning, rest, call bell

e.g. Pain 3/10. Analgesia effective. Call bell in reach.

✅ On Placement

  1. Memorise the CHIMNEOPS headings
  2. Jot notes under each domain during handover
  3. Document in real-time, not end of shift
  4. Review against checklist before handover
  5. Structure verbal handover around same domains
CHIMNEOPS for written notes, ISBAR for verbal handover. Learn both and you'll look like you know what you're doing from day one.
