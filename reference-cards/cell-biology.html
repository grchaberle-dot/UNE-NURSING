🔬 Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic

💡 Bacteria = prokaryotic. Our cells = eukaryotic. That's why antibiotics can target bacteria without destroying us.

🧱 Cell Membrane: Fluid Mosaic Model

  • Fluid: components can move within the membrane
  • Mosaic: different proteins distributed throughout
  • Selective permeability: some substances cross easily, others restricted

🧫 Membrane Transport

Passive (no energy)

  • Diffusion: high → low concentration
  • Osmosis: water toward higher solute
  • Facilitated diffusion: uses protein, no energy

Active (energy required)

  • Moves against concentration gradient
  • Uses ATP

⚖️ Homeostasis

💡 Almost every homeostatic mechanism is negative feedback. If it amplifies, it's positive feedback.

Negative feedback

Response opposes the change. Returns to set point. Dominant mechanism.

e.g. Temperature rises → sweating → cooling.

Positive feedback

Response amplifies the change. Drives to completion. Rare.

e.g. Labour contractions, blood clotting.

🧬 Cell Division & Repair

  • Mitosis: two identical daughter cells. Growth and repair.
  • Meiosis: gametes with half chromosomes. Reproduction.
  • Regeneration: same cell type replacement (ideal)
  • Fibrosis: scar tissue (functional loss)
  • High regeneration: epithelial, bone, blood
  • Low regeneration: cardiac muscle, nerve
This is why heart attacks cause permanent damage. Cardiac muscle can't regenerate, so it scars.
