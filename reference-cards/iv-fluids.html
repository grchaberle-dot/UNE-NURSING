Body Fluid Compartments, Tonicity & Which Fluid When

\uD83E\uDDEA Body Fluid Compartments

  • Intracellular fluid (ICF): fluid INSIDE the cell
  • Extracellular fluid (ECF): fluid OUTSIDE the cell, made up of:
  • Interstitial fluid (ISF): fluid surrounding the cells in the tissues
  • Intravascular (IV) fluid: plasma, the fluid inside the blood vessels
Three places fluid can sit: inside cells, around cells, or in vessels. Every IV fluid works by shifting water between those three spaces.

\u2696\uFE0F The Three Tonicities

Isotonic

Same osmolality as body fluid, so the cell stays the same.

Memory trick: Isotonic, think stays where I put it.

Hypotonic

Water leaves the vessel and enters the cell, so the cell swells.

Memory trick: HypOtonic, think goes Out of the vessel and into the cell.

Hypertonic

Water leaves the cells and enters the vessels, so the cell shrinks.

Memory trick: HypErtonic, think Enters the vessel from the cells.

\uD83E\uDDC2 Sodium: Which Tonicity When

HYPERnatraemia \u2192 use HYPOtonic

Serum sodium is HIGH, so give fluids with a LOW salt concentration to counteract it.

  • 0.45% saline (1/2 NS)
  • 0.33% saline (1/3 NS)
  • 0.225% saline (1/4 NS)

HYPOnatraemia \u2192 use HYPERtonic

Serum sodium is LOW, so give fluids with a HIGH salt concentration to replace it.

  • 5% saline
  • 3% saline
  • 5% dextrose in 0.9% saline (D5NS)
  • 5% dextrose in 0.45% saline (D5 1/2 NS)
  • 5% dextrose in LR (D5LR)
The pairing is deliberately opposite: hyper-natraemia takes hypo-tonic fluid, hypo-natraemia takes hyper-tonic fluid. Low salt problem gets high salt fluid.

Hypotonic fluids swell cells, including brain cells. That is why they are contraindicated in raised intracranial pressure.

\u2705 Quick Self-Check

  1. In hypertonic solutions, the cell shrinks.
  2. In isotonic solutions, the cell stays the same.
  3. In hypotonic solutions, the cell swells.
  4. The only fluid compatible with blood products is 0.9% sodium chloride.
  5. Hypernatraemia is treated with hypotonic fluids.
  6. D5W starts isotonic and becomes hypotonic in the body.
