🧪 Body Fluid Compartments

  • ICF: fluid inside the cell
  • ECF: fluid outside the cell (interstitial + intravascular)
Three places fluid sits: inside cells, around cells, or in vessels. IV fluids shift water between them.

⚖️ The Three Tonicities

Isotonic

Cell stays the same.

Hypotonic

Water enters cell, cell swells. HypO = Out of vessel.

Hypertonic

Water leaves cell, cell shrinks. HypEr = Enters vessel.

Same size, swells, or shrinks.

🧂 Na+ Correction

HYPERnatraemia → HYPOtonic

High Na+ → give low-salt fluid (0.45%, 0.33%)

HYPOnatraemia → HYPERtonic

Low Na+ → give high-salt fluid (3%, 5%)

The pairing is opposite: hyper gets hypo, hypo gets hyper.

🏷️ Key Rules

  • Only 0.9% NaCl is compatible with blood products
  • Hypotonic fluids are contraindicated in raised ICP
