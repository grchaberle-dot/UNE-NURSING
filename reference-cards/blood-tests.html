🩸 Blood Tube Colour Guide

Blood collection tube colour guide

Reference ranges vary between labs. Always interpret alongside your facility's intervals and the patient's full clinical picture.
Always consider the TREND (improving or deteriorating?) alongside the absolute value.

🩸 Full Blood Count (FBC)

💡 TREND matters more than a single value.

Platelets×10⁹/L150 - 450

Clinical Tips

  • Hb <100 g/L: Consider transfusion if symptomatic. Investigate cause.
  • WCC ↑: Infection/inflammation. ↓: Immunocompromised.
  • Platelets ↓ (<150): Bleeding risk. ↑ (>450): Clotting risk.

Interpretation

  • Na ↓: Hyponatraemia. Confusion, may need fluid restriction.
  • K ↑↓: Cardiac arrhythmia risk. Priority correction.
  • Elevated Cr / ↓ eGFR: Impaired renal function.
K⁺ = HEART
Potassium changes disrupt cardiac conduction. Check ECG/rhythm and escalate.

🩸 Coagulation (COAGS)

⚠️ INR >4 = bleeding danger zone.
TestReferenceUse
APTT25 - 38 secGuides heparin dosing
INR0.8 - 1.2 (therapeutic: 2-3)Warfarin monitoring
Fibrinogen1.5 - 4.0 g/LLow = bleeding risk

Key Points

  • INR on Warfarin: Goal 2-3. INR >4 = significant bleeding risk. Give IV Vitamin K or FFP.
  • APTT on Heparin: Target 60-100 sec. Check facility protocol.

Tube colours: always follow local collection requirements.
