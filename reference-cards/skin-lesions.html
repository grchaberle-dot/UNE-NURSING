\u2728 5 Functions of the Skin

1. Protection

Barrier against microorganisms (skin itself), UV rays (melanin), water loss and physical tears (keratin).

2. Regulation

Body temperature via sweating, blood vessel dilation/constriction, shivering, and goosebumps (arrector pili).

3. Absorption

O\u2082, CO\u2082, UV rays, topical medications, steroids, fat-soluble vitamins (esp. Vitamin D).

4. Synthesis

Produces melanin (UV protection, skin color), keratin (waterproofing), and Vitamin D (bone health, stimulated by UV).

5. Sensory

Nerve endings detect heat, cold, pressure, touch, vibration, and pain.

Vitamin D deficiency causes Rickets in children. The skin synthesises Vit D when stimulated by UV light. Common exam question!

\u26A0\uFE0F Skin Cancer Types

Basal Cell Carcinoma

Most common. Locally invasive, rarely metastasises. Between hairline and upper lip. Pearly-colored papule with fine telangiectasia. Treatments: curettage, surgery, cryosurgery, radiation.

Squamous Cell Carcinoma

Grows faster than basal cell, CAN metastasise. Mucous membranes, lower lip, neck, dorsum of hands. Exophytic nodules with scaling/crusting. Treatments: surgical excision, radiation.
