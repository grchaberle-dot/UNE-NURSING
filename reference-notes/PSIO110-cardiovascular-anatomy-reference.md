# PSIO110 Cardiovascular & Thoracic Anatomy: Reference Notes

> **International medical school anatomy notes (structure reference only, not for app)**

## Middle Mediastinum
- Central chest cavity, posterior to anterior mediastinum, inferior to superior mediastinum
- Division at sternal angle (manubriosternal joint to T4-T5 disc)
- Contents: heart, ascending aorta, lower SVC, pulmonary arteries/veins, primary bronchi

## Pericardial Layers (superficial to deep)
1. Fibrous pericardium
2. Parietal pericardium (serous)
3. Pericardial cavity
4. Visceral pericardium (epicardium)
5. Myocardium
6. Endocardium

**Serous pericardium = parietal + visceral**
**Phrenic nerve (C3-C5)** innervates fibrous and parietal layers

## Heart Borders & Surfaces
- **Right border:** right atrium (in line with SVC/IVC)
- **Inferior border:** mainly right ventricle
- **Left border:** left ventricle + slight left auricle
- **Anterior surface:** mainly right ventricle
- **Posterior surface:** left atrium

## Blood Flow Pathway
SVC/IVC -> RA -> tricuspid valve -> RV -> pulmonary valve -> pulmonary arteries -> lungs -> pulmonary veins -> LA -> mitral valve -> LV -> aortic valve -> aorta -> systemic circulation

## Coronary Arteries
| Artery | Branches | Supply |
|--------|----------|--------|
| RCA | PDA, right marginal | Posterior heart, SA node (60%), AV node (80%) |
| LCA -> LAD | Septal, diagonal | Anterior surface, 2/3 of heart |
| LCA -> LCX | Left marginal | Left border, posterior LA |

**Heart dominance:** determined by which artery supplies the PDA (85-90% right dominant)

## Cardiac Veins
- Great cardiac vein runs with LAD
- Middle cardiac vein runs with PDA
- Small cardiac vein runs with RCA
- All drain into coronary sinus -> right atrium

## Heart Valves & Auscultation
| Valve | Type | Auscultation |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Tricuspid | AV (3 cusps) | Left parasternal, 5th ICS |
| Mitral | AV (2 cusps) | Midclavicular, 5th ICS |
| Pulmonary | Semilunar | Left parasternal, 2nd ICS |
| Aortic | Semilunar | Right parasternal, 2nd ICS |

S1 (lub) = AV valve closure; S2 (dub) = semilunar valve closure

## Cardiac Conduction
SA node -> AV node -> Bundle of His -> L & R bundle branches -> Purkinje fibres
- Moderator band (septomarginal trabecula) carries right bundle branch
- Annulus fibrosus prevents direct atrial-ventricular conduction (AV delay)

## Sternal Angle Landmarks
- Superior/inferior mediastinum division
- Aortic arch begins and ends
- Trachea bifurcates at carina
- Azygos vein empties into SVC

## Diaphragm Openings
| Level | Structure |
|-------|-----------|
| T8 | IVC |
| T10 | Oesophagus |
| T12 | Descending aorta |

## Phrenic Nerves
- C3, C4, C5 keeps the diaphragm alive
- Travel with pericardiophrenic vessels along anterior pericardium

## Vagus Nerves (CN X)
- Parasympathetic to viscera down to transverse colon
- Recurrent laryngeal nerves innervate larynx
- Damage: unilateral = hoarseness; bilateral = near-complete airway obstruction
