# Recommended YouTube Channels for Nursing Study

> Reference resource for Georgia's UNE BNURS degree. Channels organised by what they're best for.

---

## Pathophysiology & Pharmacology

### Ninja Nerd
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@NinjaNerdOfficial
- **Best for:** Pathophysiology, pharmacology, anatomy, physiology
- **Why:** Incredibly detailed whiteboard explanations with clear visuals. The gold standard for understanding disease processes and drug mechanisms. Covers almost every system.
- **Relevant units:** PSIO215, PSIO110, PSIO120, HSNS363, HSNS372, HSNS362

### Osmosis (from Elsevier)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@osmaborana
- **Best for:** Pathophysiology, physiology, pharmacology
- **Why:** Beautiful animations that make complex processes click. Shorter videos, great for quick concept reviews. Covers pathophys, pharm, and clinical medicine.
- **Relevant units:** PSIO215, PSIO110, PSIO120, HSNS363

### Dr Matt & Dr Mike
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@DrMattDrMike
- **Best for:** Anatomy, physiology, pathophysiology, pharmacology
- **Why:** Two Aussie doctors who explain medical science in an engaging, conversational style. Great for anatomy and physiology foundations. Australian context is a bonus.
- **Relevant units:** PSIO110, PSIO120, PSIO215, HSNS363, HSNS372

## Clinical Nursing

### RegisteredNurseRN (Sarah)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@RegisteredNurseRN
- **Best for:** Clinical nursing concepts, NCLEX-style content, medications, patient care
- **Why:** Massive library covering nearly every nursing topic. Clear explanations with quizzes built in. Good for medication administration, assessment skills, and clinical reasoning.
- **Relevant units:** HSNS170, HSNS171, HSNS362, HSNS363, HSNS372, HSNS370

### Sarah Nurse
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@SarahNurse
- **Best for:** Australian nursing content, clinical skills, NMBA standards
- **Why:** Aussie nurse educator. Content aligned with Australian practice, NMBA standards, and the Australian healthcare system. Rare to find AU-specific nursing content on YouTube.
- **Relevant units:** HSNS170, HSNS171, HSNS173, HSNS374, OORA400

---

## Critical Care & Deterioration

### ICU Advantage
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@ICUadvantage
- **Best for:** ABG interpretation, ventilation, haemodynamics, critical care
- **Why:** Deep dives into critical care concepts. ABG interpretation series is outstanding. Covers ventilator management, shock, sepsis, and acute deterioration in detail.
- **Relevant units:** HSNS372, HSNS363, HSNS295

---

## Anatomy & Visualisation

### Alila Medical Media
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@AlilaMedicalMedia
- **Best for:** Anatomy animations, physiology, pathology
- **Why:** High-quality 3D medical animations. Great for visualising anatomy and physiological processes. Short, focused videos on specific topics.
- **Relevant units:** PSIO110, PSIO120, PSIO215

### Khan Academy Medicine
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@kaboranhanacademy (Medicine & Health section)
- **Best for:** Foundational physiology, biology, chemistry for health
- **Why:** Free, structured, thorough. Good for building the science foundation if you need to go back to basics. Covers cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, neuro, and more.
- **Relevant units:** PSIO110, PSIO120, PSIO215

## How to Use These

1. **Before a lecture:** Watch the Ninja Nerd or Osmosis video on the topic to prime your brain
2. **After a lecture:** Use RegisteredNurseRN or Dr Matt & Dr Mike for a different angle on the same content
3. **Exam prep:** ICU Advantage for ABGs and critical care, RegisteredNurseRN for clinical scenarios
4. **Struggling with a concept:** Try a different channel's explanation. Sometimes one style clicks where another doesn't
5. **Australian context:** Always cross-check with Sarah Nurse and your UNE unit guides for AU-specific practice
