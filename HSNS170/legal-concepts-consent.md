# Legal Concepts, Consent & Advance Directives

> ⚠️ US Source (SimpleNursing). Adapted to Australian legal framework. Verify against AU legislation.

## Torts
- Negligence: failure to provide care a reasonable nurse would
- Malpractice: negligent actions by licensed professional
- Assault: threat. Battery: harm. A before B
- False imprisonment: restraining competent patient
- Libel (written, "Library") vs Slander (spoken)

## Consent
- Patient must understand procedure, risks, benefits, alternatives
- Obtained by person performing procedure, nurse witnesses
- Cannot consent: minors, unconscious, lacking capacity

## AMA
Inform risks → Notify provider → Discuss risks → Sign form → Remove devices

## Advance Directives (AU)
Advance Care Directive, Enduring guardian, DNR/AND. No directive = full treatment.

## Mandatory Reporting (AU)
Child/elder abuse, impaired practitioners → AHPRA. Protect, interview separately, collect evidence, safety plan.
