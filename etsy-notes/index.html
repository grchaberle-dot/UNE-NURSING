\uD83C\uDFAF How to Use This Material

  • Use it to build structure: headings, topic breakdowns and revision checklists
  • Use it for memory aids: mnemonics and comparison tables are genuinely useful
  • Do not use it for clinical values without checking against Australian references and your facility's ranges
  • Do not cite it in assessments. Use it to find the real source, then cite that
  • Flag anything unverified in your own notes so you remember to check it later

\u26A0\uFE0F Known Differences to Watch

AreaWhy to be careful
Lab reference rangesVary between countries, labs and facilities. Always use your facility's intervals.
Units of measurementUS material often uses mg/dL where Australia uses mmol/L. Glucose and lipids especially.
Drug names and brandsBrand names differ between countries. Check MIMS Australia for Australian prescribing information.
Legislation and regulatorsUS material references the BRN and NCLEX. Australia has AHPRA, the NMBA and no NCLEX.
Standards frameworksAustralia uses the NSQHS Standards and NMBA Standards for Practice. US material will not mention these.
DocumentationFollow Australian and facility documentation policy, not US charting conventions.
The clinical reasoning in good US material usually transfers fine. The numbers, names and legal framework often do not.
