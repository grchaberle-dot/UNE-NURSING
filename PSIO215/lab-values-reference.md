# Lab Values Reference

> ⚠️ US Source (SimpleNursing). ALWAYS verify normal ranges against your local Australian laboratory reference ranges. Values may differ between labs.

## BMP & Electrolytes
K+ 3.5-5.0, Na+ 135-145, Cl- 97-107, Ca 9.0-10.5, Mg 1.3-2.1, Phosphate 3.0-4.5
Glucose 4-7 mmol/L (AU), BUN, Creatinine

## CBC
WBC, RBC, Hgb, Hct, Platelets (150-400k)

## Coagulation
PT, INR (therapeutic 2.0-3.0 for warfarin), aPTT (heparin monitoring)

## Cardiac
Troponin (elevated = myocardial injury), BNP (elevated = heart failure), CK-MB

## Toxic Drug Levels
Digoxin, Lithium, Theophylline, Aminoglycosides, Vancomycin: narrow therapeutic index, monitor levels closely

## Priority Labs
Critical values requiring immediate notification: K+ <3.0 or >6.0, Na+ <120 or >160, glucose <3.0 or >25, Hgb <70, platelets <50k, INR >5.0, troponin positive
