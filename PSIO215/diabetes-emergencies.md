# Diabetes Emergencies & Complications

## DKA
Insulin deficiency → fat breakdown → ketones → metabolic acidosis. Kussmaul breathing, fruity breath.

## HHS
Enough insulin to prevent ketones but not hyperglycaemia. Very high BGL, severe dehydration. Neuro signs mimic stroke.

## Hypoglycaemia
Too little glucose for brain. Rule of fifteen. Check BGL, fast-acting glucose, recheck.

## Complications
Macrovascular (stroke, MI, PVD). Microvascular (retinopathy, neuropathy, nephropathy, 10-20yr post dx)
