# Electrolyte Imbalances

> ⚠️ US Source (SimpleNursing). Verify normal ranges against local AU lab references.

## Mnemonics
- K+ Pumps the heart (PRIORITY)
- Na+ Swells the body with fluid
- Mg+ Mellows the muscles
- Ca Contracts muscles, 3 Bs (Bone, Blood, Beats)
- Phosphate: inverse to calcium

## Key Imbalances
- HyperK: peaked T waves, VF, cardiac arrest. Causes: renal failure
- HypoK: flat T waves, shallow resp (MOST DEADLY), paralytic ileus
- HypoNa: cerebral oedema, seizures, coma. Causes: SIADH, excess water
- HyperNa: oedema, flushed skin. Causes: low ADH (DI = Dry Inside)
- HypoCa: Trousseau's, Chvostek's, tetany, weak Bs
- HyperCa: stones, moans, groans, constipation, bone pain
- HypoMg: Torsades de Pointes (TOP TESTED), VF

## IV Solutions
- Isotonic ("I-SO-perfect"): 0.9% NS, LR. No fluid shift
- Hypotonic ("HippO-tonic"): 0.45% NS, D5W. Cells swell. NOT for raised ICP
- Hypertonic ("HYPER-tonic"): 3% NS, D5NS. Cells shrink
