# Cardiac Pharmacology

## LAP (Ventricular Rhythm Drugs)
- L: Lidocaine (1st line VT/VF, neuro checks priority)
- A: Amiodarone (2nd line, pulmonary toxicity risk)
- P: Procainamide (less common)

## Key Drugs
- Atropine: symptomatic bradycardia
- Adenosine: SVT (rapid IV push, briefly stops heart)
- Adrenaline: cardiac arrest every 3-5 min, anaphylaxis IM 0.5mg
- Noradrenaline: 1st line vasopressor for septic shock
- Digoxin: positive inotrope, narrow therapeutic index, hold if HR <60
- GTN: vasodilator for angina/MI, NEVER with sildenafil

## Effects
- Inotropic: contractility
- Chronotropic: heart rate
- Dromotropic: conduction speed
