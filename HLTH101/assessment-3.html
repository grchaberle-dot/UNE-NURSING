🎯 The Task

You post one forum response for each of Modules 1–6. For A3, pick TWO of your three posts from Modules 4, 5 or 6 and write a critical reflection essay.

Draft every forum post in Word and SAVE it. Keep the Mod 4–6 posts tight, because they eat into your 900.
