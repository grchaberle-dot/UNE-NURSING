🎯 The Task

Across the trimester you post one forum response for each of Modules 1–6. For A3, pick TWO of your three posts from Modules 4, 5 or 6 and write a critical reflection.

Draft every forum post in Word and SAVE it. The brief has you paste the posts in from "your original Word doc".
