1. Fact vs Opinion

  • Is an opinion based on proof, unsubstantiated belief, or social media yesterday?
  • Never mistake opinion for fact, e.g. when documenting patient/client care
  • Video 1: fact vs opinion explainer (a CBT adjunct tool)

2. Q&A: Experts + Empirical Evidence (ABC)

  • Opinion presented as fact; fact disputed as opinion
  • Experiential/idiosyncratic evidence shapes opinions
  • Red flag: call to authority (name-dropping scientific institutions)
  • Red flag: "irrefutable" claims with little or no data
  • Who do we listen to, and who should make decisions?

3. Manipulated Evidence

  • Large industries manipulate data for advantage and profit
  • US tobacco and alcohol advertising distorts perceptions
  • Spot it: industry denials contradict their own ads
