✨ What This Unit Is About

In modern health and social care, actions and advice have to rest on reliable, up-to-date information and evidence. HLTH101 builds the foundation skills for using information and evidence in health and social care practice:

  • Identifying sources of information and evidence
  • Looking at how research and evidence-based practice (EBP) have improved outcomes for individuals and communities
  • Weighing up the strengths, limitations and challenges of an evidence-based approach
  • Tracing how information and evidence evolved and shaped health professional education and practice
  • Using a structured approach to locate, collate and present information and evidence in the right format for the context
It's interdisciplinary: nursing, social work, community services, psych, community welfare, enabling/TRACKS and OUA students all take it. That mix is deliberate, it's practice for multidisciplinary teamwork.

🎯 Learning Outcomes

By the end of the unit you should be able to:

  1. identify and evaluate sources of information and evidence for health professional learning and practice
  2. describe the evolution of information and evidence in health and social care and its impact on the development of health professional education and practice
  3. provide examples of how information and evidence has resulted in research and evidence based practice for improved outcomes for individuals and communities
  4. demonstrate a beginning ability to initiate inquiry and reflection in their chosen profession
  5. use a structured approach to locate and collate information and evidence
  6. present information and evidence in contextually appropriate ways in a range of formats
