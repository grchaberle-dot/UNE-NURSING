🔍 Source Pointers: Dalton (2026)

Pointers to what's IN the article. The evaluation is yours to write.

  • Publication: masthead and section tags (National / NSW / Autism). What type of publication is it, who's it written for, and is there any sign of peer review?
  • Content: headline tone ("Trump blamed autism on this pain pill. Now, the gold-standard evidence is in"). It reports a Lancet review (43 studies reviewed, 17 re-analysed, sibling comparisons) through a hyperlink. Is there a reference list? Can you trace the claim back to its source? There's also a KFF Tracking poll stat.
  • Who's quoted: Dr Anya Arthurs (Uni of Adelaide), Prof Julie Leask (Uni of Sydney) and Dr Chris Edwards (Aspect, autistic himself). What does each add, and does anyone bring a potential bias?
  • Author: byline says Angus Dalton is "the science reporter for The Sydney Morning Herald". Qualifications? Paid?
  • Conclusion: how reliable and valid is it for a health professional, compared with the Lancet paper it reports on?
