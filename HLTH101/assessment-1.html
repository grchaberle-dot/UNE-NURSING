🎯 The Task

  • Parker (2026)

  • Kamdar (2026)
  • D'Antonio et al. (2026)

📄 Page 1: The Analysis Grid

One column per source, four rows:

RowWhat to cover
The Publication / SourceType of publication, intended audience, peer reviewed or not
The ContentWriting style/tone; referenced comprehensively, partially or not at all; where the info came from and how easily you can verify it
The AuthorQualifications, affiliation/s, other publications, bias, whether they were paid (or paid to be published)
ConclusionHow reliable and valid is it as a source for you as a current/developing health professional?

📄 Page 2: Validity, Reliability, References

Validity box

A profession-relevant definition, plus examples of why validity matters to your health profession when you evaluate information.

Reliability box

A profession-relevant definition, plus examples of why reliability matters to your health profession when you evaluate information.

References

APA 7th, as the final section of the poster. You also need to find extra appropriate sources to back up your analysis (the Reading List has a few to start with).
