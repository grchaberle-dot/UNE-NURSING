1. What is EBP?

Using the best available, most appropriate evidence to make healthcare decisions for our patients (Zadvinskis et al., 2022).

  1. Rigorous scientific evidence
  2. Clinical expertise
  3. Patient's needs, values + circumstances
All three are now equally important. Clinical judgement decides if research suits THIS patient. Goal: minimise error + uncertainty.

2. History + Benefits

  • Started 1970s as evidence-based medicine; now across all health professions
  • Better quality + cost of care, patient outcomes, staff satisfaction
  • Requires being professional, responsible + accountable

3. Criticisms

  • Not all professionals engage consistently (Melnyk and colleagues have researched this in nursing for 10+ years)
  • Time consuming to find evidence → TRIP (Turning Research into Practice): fast search, study categories, patient info leaflets
  • Limited/inconsistent research → decide on clinical expertise + patient preference
