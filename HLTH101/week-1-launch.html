💬 Module 1 Forum Activity

For a health leaflet, compare four possible sources: library textbooks, database journal articles, Wikipedia and ChatGPT. Give one advantage and one disadvantage for each.

  • Length: 100 words, plus or minus 10% (90 to 110 words).
  • Referencing: in-text citations, at least 1 reference, APA 7th.
  • Workflow: draft and save it in Word, then paste it into the forum. Do not attach the file.
  • You must post and mark it off before moving to the next forum.

🧠 Tiny scaffold

  1. Make a 4 by 2 grid: one plus and one minus for each source.
  2. Anchor each point to a CRACKER idea: relevance, currency, reliability, accuracy or coverage.
  3. Keep it tight: roughly 20 to 25 words per source.
  4. Check the word count and APA formatting, then post it.

This is a learning snapshot, not a perfect final answer. Your thinking will change as the unit progresses.
