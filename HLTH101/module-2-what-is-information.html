🎯 Learning Objectives (ULO 2, 3 + 6)

  • Describe + discuss the history and evolution of information
  • Understand how information is disseminated
  • Analyse the impact of information systems in a healthcare setting
  • Present information in the academic context

1. History + Evolution of Information

  • Recording, retrieval + interpretation of info is as old as humankind, limited only by the technology of the time
  • From cave paintings and rock carvings to computers, the purpose is mostly the same: preserve info to pass on to future generations
  • Gunn et al. (2022): rock art of the Unghangho family of the Kwini people, Balanggarra country, added to over thousands of years, sequentially recording experience, beliefs and culture

2. Built on Past Technology

Milestones: printing press → postal system → electricity → wireless → transistors → microchips (process began well before the Industrial Revolution)

Orthia (2020): scientific communication + inclusiveness, beyond treating "science" as the domain of relatively recent Western civilisations

3. Information Overload

Easy access to info on almost any topic has advantages, but you need to critically evaluate and identify key sources to avoid overload.

This is Module 1 in action: CRACKER, peer review, reliability + validity.

4. Paper Files → Electronic Records

  • Electronic records are now commonplace in healthcare
  • Advantages and disadvantages for both clinicians and patients
  • One perspective: Kariotis et al. (2022)

5. IST + the Maturity Model

  • Gradual development of Information Systems Technology (IST), especially in healthcare
  • A maturity model measures progress
  • Article is relatively old: find a more current one and see if thinking has changed as technology evolved
