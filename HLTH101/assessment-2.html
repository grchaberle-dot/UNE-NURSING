🎯 The Task

Read the Taylor and Brown Case Study and Scenarios. The families ask you two questions. Pick ONE:

  • Does the MMR vaccine cause autism?
  • Is facilitated communication a valid intervention?

Answer it, and detail and justify your answer using critical thinking, valid academic literature, in-text citations and references. Start with the paper links on myLearn.

Their hint: "go back to Module 1 to check you are on the right track!"

👪 The Case Study

In both scenarios you're a health professional supporting Alison and Paul (Alison Taylor married Paul Brown, NW Sydney, kids Blake 5 and Sarah 3).

Scenario 1: Vaccination

  • Sarah (3) can't be vaccinated (allergies and a compromised immune system) and gets sick often. Blake (5) is up to date.
  • Alison fears measles and/or whooping cough and wants no contact with Emily's unvaccinated kids, Jessica (8) and Paula (5).
  • Emily is anti-vax because the family links Edward's autism (delay at 3, autism confirmed at 5) to his MMR.
  • Alison asks: does the MMR vaccine cause autism?

Scenario 2: Facilitated Communication

  • Joan and Eric use FC with Edward and believe it helps (plus sight cards and picture stories).
  • Paul has strong doubts, but wonders if it could help Adam (severe brain injury 3 years ago, severe communication and mobility difficulties) and Kyra (5, developmental delay).
  • Rachel (Adam's wife, Kyra's grandmother) leans on Paul for support.
  • Paul asks: how effective is FC as an intervention tool?
Think about your audience (Alison or Paul) and what they actually need from you. In Scenario 1 the stakes are a child who CAN'T be vaccinated. In Scenario 2 FC might be applied to two very different people.
