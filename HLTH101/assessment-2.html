🎯 The Task

Read the Taylor and Brown Case Study. Pick ONE question and answer it, detailing and justifying your answer:

  • Does the MMR vaccine cause autism? → start with Patil (2011)
  • Is facilitated communication a valid intervention? → start with Biklen (1990)
"Correctly locating the original research is part of your assessment." Get it wrong and slides 1–3 drop to the Fail band. Go back to Module 1!

👪 The Case Study

In both scenarios you're a health professional supporting Alison and Paul (Alison Taylor married Paul Brown, NW Sydney, kids Blake 5 and Sarah 3).

Scenario 1: Vaccination

  • Sarah (3) can't be vaccinated (allergies, compromised immunity), gets sick often. Blake is up to date.
  • Alison fears measles/whooping cough and wants no contact with Emily's unvaccinated kids.
  • Emily is anti-vax because the family links Edward's autism to MMR.
  • Alison asks: does MMR cause autism?

Scenario 2: Facilitated Communication

  • Joan and Eric use FC with Edward (plus sight cards and picture stories).
  • Paul doubts it, but wonders about it for Adam (brain injury) and Kyra (developmental delay).
  • Paul asks: how effective is FC?
