1. Information vs Evidence

  • Information evolves with communication
  • Evidence evolves with science and enquiry
  • Evidence = information developed via the process of enquiry

2. Birth of Western Enquiry

  • Enquiry traces to early humans: tools and fire
  • Western scientific enquiry: the Greeks, 470 BCE, the Socratic Method + falsification
  • Socrates → Plato → Aristotle: note their comments and how they relate to now
Read Dwyer (2017) Ch 3, Critical thinking: Conceptual perspectives and practical guidelines.

3. Scientific Method + EBP

  1. Prior body of knowledge
  2. Hypothesis (starting point / initial explanation)
  3. Tested by empirical methods (observable + measurable)
  4. Results = evidence base for further research
EBP is the gold standard. Scientist practitioner = proven method, research-based, validated, reliable.
