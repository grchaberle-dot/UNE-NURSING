# Wound Care & Pressure Injuries

> ⚠️ US Source. Staging is universal. Verify wound care products/protocols locally.

## Stages
1 = epidermis (red, non-blanchable). 2 = epidermis+dermis (open). 3 = into subcut (tunnelling). 4 = muscle/bone/tendon. Unstageable = eschar/slough. Deep tissue = dark purple.

## Prevention
Braden Scale, turn 1-2hr, protein, albumin 3.5-5.0, fluids 2-3L/day

## Debridement
Mechanical, Enzymatic, Surgical, Tangential, Fascial
