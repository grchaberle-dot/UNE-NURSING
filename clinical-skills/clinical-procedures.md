# Clinical Procedures Guide

## NG Tube
Purpose: decompress stomach, nutrition, irrigate. Measure nose-ear-xiphoid. Confirm with X-ray. pH ~4.

## IDC (Urinary Catheter)
Sterile procedure. Insert until urine flows + advance 2-3cm. Inflate balloon with NS. Clamp if >1000mL.

## Ostomy Care
Healthy stoma = juicy red. Cut appliance 1/8 inch larger. Barrier cream 2 inches from stoma.

## CVCs
Tunnelled (long-term, heparin flush), PICC (basilic vein, phlebitis common), Vascular access port (implanted, non-coring needle), Epidural (analgesia)

## Blood Transfusions
2 nurses verify. Baseline vitals. Start slowly 15 min. Reaction: STOP, NS, notify team.

## PPE
Don: HH → Gown → Mask → Goggles → Gloves. Remove: Gloves → Goggles → Gown → Mask → HH

## Precautions
- Airborne (MTV): Measles, TB, Varicella
- Droplet (SPIDERMAN): Sepsis, Parvovirus, Influenza, Diphtheria, Epiglottitis, Rubella, Meningitis, Adenovirus, Mumps
- Contact: MDR organisms, skin/wound/enteric/eye infections
