# Patient Positioning

> ⚠️ US Source (SimpleNursing). Verify against AU placement protocols.

## Positions
- Fowler's (40-90°): cardiac, SOB, NG tube
- Lateral: GI issues, rectal surgery, recovery position
- Lithotomy: gynaecological, childbirth
- Sim's: enemas, perineal exams, pregnancy
- Prone: drainage after oral/neck surgery
- Supine: natural at-rest, abdominal/facial procedures
- Trendelenburg: hypotension, central line insertion
- Reverse Trendelenburg: perfusion in obese, venous air embolism

## 5 Rights of Delegation
Right task, circumstance, person, direction/communication, supervision/evaluation
