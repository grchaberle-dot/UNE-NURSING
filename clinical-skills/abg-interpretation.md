# ABG Interpretation & Acid-Base Balance

## Normal Values
pH 7.35-7.45, PaCO2 35-45, HCO3 22-26, PaO2 80-100

## ROME Mnemonic
Respiratory = Opposite (pH and PaCO2 opposite). Metabolic = Equal (pH and HCO3 same direction)

## 4 Imbalances
- Respiratory acidosis: pH LOW, PaCO2 HIGH (COPD, sedation, pneumonia)
- Respiratory alkalosis: pH HIGH, PaCO2 LOW (hyperventilation, anxiety, pain)
- Metabolic acidosis: pH LOW, HCO3 LOW (DKA, renal failure, lactic acidosis)
- Metabolic alkalosis: pH HIGH, HCO3 HIGH (vomiting, NG suction, diuretics)

## Compensation
Respiratory problem → kidneys adjust HCO3 (slow). Metabolic problem → lungs adjust CO2 (fast)

## Kussmaul Breathing
Deep rapid respirations in metabolic acidosis (DKA). Body blowing off CO2 to compensate.
