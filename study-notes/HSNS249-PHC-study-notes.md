# HSNS249 Primary Health Care & Health Promotion

> Source: UNE HSNS246 Workshop Week 5 (official)

## Key Definitions

- **Health (WHO):** Complete physical, mental and social wellbeing, not merely absence of disease
- **Primary Health Care (Alma-Ata):** Essential health care based on scientifically sound, socially acceptable methods, universally accessible through full participation
- **Health Equity:** Universal access best achieved through equity, not equality
- **Declaration of Astana (2018):** Commitment to use scientific AND traditional knowledge

## PHC Principles
Cultural sensitivity, intersectoral collaboration, equitable access, health promotion

## Prevention Levels
1. **Primary:** First line of care, maintain health, reduce/eliminate risk factors
2. **Secondary:** Screening, early detection, treating and limiting illness
3. **Tertiary:** Rehabilitative/restorative actions for established disease

## Ottawa Charter
- **5 Action Areas:** Reorient health services, build healthy public policy, strengthen community action, create supportive environments, develop personal skills
- **3 Strategies:** Advocate, enable (health equity), mediate

## Health Promotion Pillars (WHO 2016)
Good Governance, Healthy Cities, Health Literacy

## Cultural Concepts
- **Cultural awareness:** Recognising differences, guarding against own biases as norm
- **Cultural safety:** Actions that recognise and nurture unique cultural identity
- **Cultural competence:** Sensitivity to similarities/differences in effective communication
- **Country (Aboriginal):** Relationship to family, community and land determining lore and meaning
