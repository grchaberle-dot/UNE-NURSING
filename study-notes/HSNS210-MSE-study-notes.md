# HSNS210 Mental State Examination & Risk Assessment

> Source: UNE HSNS310/510 Topic 4 Lecture (official)

## Mental State Examination (MSE)

Process of obtaining information about an individual's mental experiences and behaviour at the time of interview. Must be documented professionally. "If it's not documented, it was not done."

### 10 Components

1. **Appearance:** age, gender, build, hygiene, dress, physical features
2. **Behaviour:** eye contact, motor activity, expressive gestures
3. **Speech:** articulation (slurred, stammering), rate (rapid, pressured, slow), volume, quantity
4. **Mood & Affect:** Mood = subjective/internal (client's words). Affect = objective/observable. Described by range (normal, restricted, blunted, flat), stability (stable, labile, diurnal variation), and appropriateness/congruity
5. **Thought Form:** loosening of associations, tangentiality, circumstantiality, flight of ideas, perseveration, thought blocking, neologisms
6. **Thought Content:** suicidal thoughts, harm to others, delusions (persecution, reference, control, grandiose, guilt, religious, nihilistic, jealousy, somatic)
7. **Perceptual Disturbances:** hallucinations (auditory, visual, olfactory, gustatory, tactile), derealisation, depersonalisation, illusions
8. **Sensorium & Cognition:** consciousness level, memory, orientation (time/place/person), concentration (serial 7s), abstract thought, MMSE
9. **Insight:** recognise own problems, medication knowledge, amenability to treatment
10. **Formulation:** summary of signs, symptoms, risk, strengths/weaknesses

## Risk Assessment

### Types of Risk
- **To self:** suicide, self-harm, exploitation, substance abuse, self-neglect, absconding
- **To others:** violence/homicide, harm to child, fire setting

### Suicide Risk Levels
- **No risk:** no past/current risk, protective factors: hourly visual obs
- **Low:** some risk factors, no current risk: 30 min visual obs
- **Moderate:** definite current risk, no plan: 15 min close visual obs
- **High:** clear plan, immediate/near future: one-to-one continuous obs

### Screening Tools
- **CAGE** (alcohol): Cut down, Annoyed, Guilty, Eye-opener. 2+ yes = investigate
- **K10** (Kessler): anxiety/depression distress. Max 50 (severe), min 10 (no distress)
- **HCR-20:** Historical Clinical Risk Management Scale
