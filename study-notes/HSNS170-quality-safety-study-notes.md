## 1. NMBA NP Safety & Quality Guidelines

The NMBA regulates nursing and midwifery practice under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law. The Safety and Quality Guidelines for Nurse Practitioners (updated November 2024) outline the regulatory requirements NPs must meet.

### Scope of Practice
- NP scope is built on the RN scope and must meet NMBA standards, Code of Conduct, and ICN Code of Ethics.
- NPs expanding or changing scope must use the NMBA Decision-Making Framework.
- Both NP and employer share responsibility for ensuring competence.

### Context of Practice
Type of setting, location (urban/rural/remote), consumer characteristics, focus of activities, degree of autonomy, available resources.

### Standards for Practice
NPs must meet the Nurse Practitioner Standards for Practice (2021) for endorsement, eligibility, return to work, and ongoing competence.

### Annual Declaration & Audit
NPs declare annually at registration renewal. NMBA and Ahpra audit compliance.

### Mandatory Reporting
Section 140 National Law: report notifiable conduct to Ahpra.

### Professional Indemnity Insurance
Section 129(1): NPs must have appropriate PII for practice.

### Recency of Practice
450 hours direct clinical contact over past 5 years at advanced practice level.

### CPD
20hrs general + 10hrs NP-specific per year (prescribing, diagnostics, consultation/referral).

### Medicare & PBS
NPs apply as participating NPs under Health Insurance Act 1973. Endorsement does NOT give automatic MBS/PBS access.

### Prescribing Authority
Conferred under state/territory drugs and poisons legislation.

### Key Definitions
- Advanced practice: 5,000 hours clinically-based in past 6 years
- Nurse Practitioner: RN endorsed by NMBA, practises independently with direct clinical contact
- Scope of practice: full spectrum of roles individuals are educated, competent and authorised to perform

## 2. Person-Centred Care

### Principles
Education, informed participation, family involvement, collaboration, respect for values, effective information flow, dignity.

### Eight Dimensions
1. Respect for preferences and values
2. Emotional support
3. Physical comfort
4. Information, communication and education
5. Continuity and transition
6. Coordination of care
7. Involvement of family and friends
8. Access to care

### Therapeutic Communication
Active listening, clarification, silence. Empathy fuels connection; sympathy drives disconnection. Four qualities: perspective taking, staying out of judgement, recognising emotions, communicating understanding.

---

## 3. Patient Safety Systems

### Australian Safety & Quality Framework
1. Consumer-centred
2. Driven by information and evidence
3. Organised for safety

### Systems Thinking
Shift from individual blame to examining wider systems. Human factors: fatigue, interruption, confusing systems. Redesign work conditions.

### Safety Tools
Checklists, risk assessments, standardised handover, incident reporting, medication safety, electronic records, decision support, policies, staffing, audits.

---

## 4. Clinical Reasoning

### Five Rights
Right cues, right patient, right action, right time, right reason.

### Clinical Reasoning Cycle
Looking, collecting, processing, deciding, planning, acting, evaluating.

### Documentation
Narrative, structured, exception, electronic. Must be legible, accurate, dated, signed, contemporaneous.

### Shared Decision-Making
Clinician and patient jointly decide. Discuss options, risks/benefits, preferences, values.

## 8. Ethics & Professional Boundaries

Autonomy, non-maleficence, beneficence, justice, veracity, advocacy. ICN Code of Ethics (4 elements). Professional boundaries. Mental Health First Aid (5 actions).

## 9. Falls, Pressure Injuries & Safe Transfers

Braden Scale (6 categories, score <=9 = very high risk). Stages 1-4, unstageable, deep tissue injury. Prevention: reposition q2h, clean/dry skin, nutrition, transfer aids. Safe body mechanics.

## 10. Rural & Remote Health

Geographic isolation, limited infrastructure, fewer professionals, financial barriers. Telehealth, RFDS, cultural safety.
