## 1. Heart Chambers & Valves

- **Name the four chambers:** Right Atrium, Right Ventricle, Left Atrium, Left Ventricle
- **Name the four valves:** Tricuspid, Pulmonary, Mitral (Bicuspid), Aortic
- **Thickest wall:** Left Ventricle (systemic pressure)
- **Blood flow pathway:** Vena cava → RA → Tricuspid → RV → Pulmonary valve → PA → Lungs → Pulm veins → LA → Mitral → LV → Aortic valve → Aorta

## 2. Cardiac Cycle & Sounds

- **S1 (lub):** AV valves closing at start of systole
- **S2 (dub):** Semilunar valves closing at start of diastole
- **CO formula:** HR x SV
- **Normal CO:** ~5 L/min
- **Systole:** Contraction/ejection
- **Diastole:** Relaxation/filling

## 3. Muscle Types

- **Skeletal:** Voluntary, striated, multinucleate, bone-attached
- **Cardiac:** Involuntary, striated, intercalated discs, heart only
- **Smooth:** Involuntary, not striated, single nucleus, hollow organs
- **Sarcomere:** Basic contractile unit
- **Myosin:** Thick filaments
- **Actin:** Thin filaments

## 5. Skin Layers & Wound Healing

- **Layers:** Epidermis → Dermis → Hypodermis
- **Healing phases:** Haemostasis → Inflammation → Proliferation → Maturation

## 6. Respiratory System

- **External respiration:** Alveoli ↔ pulmonary capillaries
- **Internal respiration:** Systemic capillaries ↔ tissue cells
- **O2 transport:** 98.5% on haemoglobin
- **CO2 transport:** 70% as bicarbonate
- **Type 1 resp failure:** Low PaO2, normal/low PaCO2
- **Type 2 resp failure:** High PaCO2 +/- low PaO2

## 7. GI Tract

- **Pathway:** Mouth → Pharynx → Oesophagus → Stomach → Duodenum → Jejunum → Ileum → Large intestine → Rectum → Anus
- **Main absorption:** Small intestine (jejunum)
