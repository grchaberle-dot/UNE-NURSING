# HSNS372 Acute Deterioration & Clinical Nursing: Study Notes

> **Source**: Studocu (unverified, cross-check with UNE materials)

---

## 1. Recognising Acute Deterioration

Core obs: RR, SpO2, HR, BP, temp, sedation/consciousness. Identify trends not single observations.

### Why Deterioration Is Missed
Incomplete obs, changes not communicated, no formal system, lack of skills, not escalated.

### Escalation
White (routine), Pink (senior review), Purple (emergency). Always escalate when concerned, score increasing, or not reviewed in time.

---

## 2. ABCDE Primary Survey

### A: Airway
Ask/look/listen/feel. Suction, head-tilt chin-lift, jaw thrust, adjuncts.

### B: Breathing (RATES)
Respiratory rate, Auscultation (6 fields), Trachea (central), Effort/expansion, Saturation. High Fowler's position.

### C: Circulation
Cap refill, skin colour/temp, pulse, BP (bilateral if indicated), IV access, UO, fluid balance, ECG. BP difference >20mmHg between arms may indicate aortic dissection.

### D: Disability
AVPU, GCS, pupils (PEARL), PQRST pain, BSL, medications, reversible causes.

### E: Exposure
Head-to-toe, privacy/dignity/warmth, spinal precautions.

---

## 3. ISOBAR

Identify, Situation, Observations, Background, Assessment/agreed plan, Readback.

---

## 4. BLS & DRSABCD

Danger, Response, Send for help, Airway, Breathing, CPR (30:2), Defibrillation.

### 4H4T
Hypoxia, Hypovolaemia, Electrolytes/metabolic, Hyper/hypothermia, Tension pneumo, Tamponade, Toxins, Thrombosis.

---

## 5. Respiratory

Breath sounds: stridor (upper airway), wheeze (narrowed), crackles (oedema/pneumonia), absent (urgent). ABGs: interpret together. Asthma action plan + spacer technique. COPD: chronic bronchitis vs emphysema, spirometry.

---

## 6. Shock

Hypovolaemic, Cardiogenic, Septic, Anaphylactic, Neurogenic, Obstructive. Sepsis: elevated lactate = deterioration.

---

## 7. ACS & Cardiac

PQRST pain assessment. MONA (morphine, oxygen, nitrates, aspirin). CO = SV x HR. ECG components. Rate: 300/large squares or 6-sec strip x10.

---

## 8. Arrhythmias

Shockable: VT (pulseless), VF. Non-shockable: asystole, PEA.

---

## 9. High-Risk Meds

GTN: vasodilator, monitor BP. Amiodarone: glucose only, central line preferred, 2-nurse check. Infusion calcs: dose/min = ordered x weight, rate = dose per hr / concentration.

---

## 10. Neuro, Seizures & Stroke

AVPU, GCS, pupils, limb movement. Seizure: stay, time, lateral position, don't restrain. Status epilepticus >5min. Swallow screen before oral intake.

---

## 11. CVADs

PICC, CVC, Hickman, Broviac, Vascath. Assess before each access, 8-hourly. Dressing change with chlorhexidine. Complications: air embolism, thrombosis, migration, occlusion, pneumothorax.

---

## 12. Medication Safety

6 rights: patient, medication, dose, route, time, documentation. Never conceal errors.

---

## 13. Pharmacology Patterns

-olol (beta blockers), -pril (ACE-I), -sartan (ARB), -statin (HMG-CoA), -dipine (CCB), -eprazole (PPI), -azepam (benzos), -cillin (penicillins), cef- (cephalosporins), -xaban (Factor Xa), -semide (loop diuretics).
