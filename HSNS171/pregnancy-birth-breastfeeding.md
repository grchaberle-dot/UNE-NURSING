# Pregnancy, Birth & Breastfeeding

## Hormones
FSH (follicle growth), LH (triggers ovulation), Oestrogen (endometrium), Progesterone (maintains endometrium)
Prolactin (milk production), Oxytocin (let-down + uterine contraction)

## Timeline
40 weeks from LMP. T1: 1-12, T2: 13-27, T3: 28-birth. Full term: 39-40 weeks.

## Breastfeeding
Colostrum (first days, immune components). Hindmilk (later, higher fat). WHO: exclusive 6 months.
Alcohol follows blood alcohol; expressing doesn't remove it faster.

## IVF
Stimulation → Monitoring → Egg retrieval → Sperm retrieval → Fertilisation → Culture → Transfer → Test
