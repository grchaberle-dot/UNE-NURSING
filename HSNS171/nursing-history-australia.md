# History of Nursing in Australia

## Key Timeline
- 1816: Sydney Infirmary opens
- 1838: First trained nurses arrive (Irish Sisters of Charity)
- 1868: Lucy Osborne, Lady Superintendent
- 1902: May Yarrowick, first professional Aboriginal nurse
- 1928: Royal Flying Doctor Service
- 1942: Vivian Bullwinkel, Banka Island Massacre survivor
- 1971: Sally Goold establishes Aboriginal Medical Services
- 1980s: Shift from hospital to university training
