# Injection Techniques: IM, Subcut & Intradermal

> ⚠️ US Source (SimpleNursing). Use for technique and anatomy only. Verify against Australian guidelines.

## IM Injections
- 90 degree angle, Z-track technique
- Sites: ventrogluteal (preferred), vastus lateralis (infants), deltoid (small volume)
- Aspiration NOT required per current AU guidelines
- Wait 10 sec, withdraw smoothly, gentle pressure, do NOT massage

## Subcutaneous
- 45-90 degrees depending on tissue
- Sites: abdomen, upper arm, thigh. Rotate.
- Used for insulin, heparin, some vaccines

## Intradermal
- 5-15 degrees (nearly flat)
- Inner forearm. Should form a wheal.
- Used for allergy/TB testing. Do NOT massage.
