# Oxygenation & Respiratory Assessment

> ⚠️ US Source. O2 devices universal. Verify local protocols.

## Lung Anatomy
Trachea → Bronchus → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli (gas exchange)
R lung 3 lobes, L lung 2 lobes. Listen in intercostal spaces.

## Auscultation
Anterior: top down, compare sides. Posterior: lean forward, best for fluid.
Normal = vesicular (soft, low-pitched, breezy)

## O2 Devices
Nasal cannula 1-6L, Simple mask 5-8L, Non-rebreather 10-15L, Venturi (precise), BiPAP (non-invasive)

## IV Complications
Infiltration (cool/pale), Phlebitis (red/warm), Extravasation (EMERGENCY), Air embolism (Trendelenburg left lateral)
