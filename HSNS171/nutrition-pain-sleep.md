# Nutrition, Pain, Sleep & Comfort

> ⚠️ US Source. Verify medication names against AU guidelines.

## Nutrition
Assess diet history, illnesses, meds, cultural needs. Carbs/fats/protein/vitamins/minerals/water.
Diets: clear liquid → full liquid → soft → regular → therapeutic

## Pain
Assess: 0-10 scale, SOCRATES. Multimodal: paracetamol → NSAIDs → opioids + non-pharm.
Reassess 30min IV, 60min oral.

## Sleep
Stages 1-3 + REM (90min cycles). Disorders: insomnia (safety priority), sleep apnoea (central vs obstructive), narcolepsy.

## Asepsis
Medical vs surgical. 5 moments hand hygiene. Soap and water for C. diff. Sterile field rules.
