# Medication Routes & Injection Technique

## Routes
Oral, IV, IM, subcutaneous, intradermal, transdermal, intrathecal, vaginal, ophthalmic, otic, nasal, sublingual

## 6 Rights
Right patient, dose, route, medication, time, documentation

## 3 Checks
1st: gathering from med room (check MAR)
2nd: preparing medication (MAR, expiry, calculations)
3rd: at bedside before giving (MAR + patient ID)

## IM Sites
Deltoid (small volume), Ventrogluteal (preferred adults), Vastus lateralis (infants/children)

## IV Push Safety
POTASSIUM IS NEVER GIVEN IV PUSH (causes cardiac arrest)
