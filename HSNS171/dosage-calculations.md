# Dosage Calculations 101

## Desired/Have Formula
Dose = (Desired / Have) x Vehicle

## Unit Conversions
mcg → mg → g → kg: moving UP = divide by 1000, moving DOWN = multiply by 1000

## IV Drip Rate
Drops/min = (Volume mL x Drop factor) / Time in minutes

## IV Pump Rate
mL/hr = Volume / Hours

## Weight-Based Dosing
Dose = Weight (kg) x Prescribed dose per kg

## Rounding
5 and above = round up. 4 and below = stays the same.
