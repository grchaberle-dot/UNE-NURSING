# Mental Health Assessment & Legislation (NSW)

## GETCP Assessment
- General behaviour/appearance
- Emotional state (mood vs affect)
- Thought context/processes
- Cognitive ability
- Physical functioning

## Care Levels
CL1 (constant view) through CL5 (every 2 hours)

## Mental Health Act 2007 (NSW)
- Voluntary: admits of own free will, discharged on written request
- Involuntary: meets strict criteria (mental illness needing treatment, can't make decisions, danger to self/others)
- ITO: involuntary treatment order
- CTO: community treatment order (approved by Mental Health Review Tribunal)

## Tribunal Panel
Lawyer, psychiatrist, another qualified member
