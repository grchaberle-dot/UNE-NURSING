# Mental Health Conditions & Medications

## Conditions
- Psychosis: disconnection from reality (symptom, not illness)
- Schizophrenia: positive (hallucinations, delusions) + negative (flat, withdrawn)
- Bipolar: extreme mood shifts, mania vs hypomania
- Personality disorders: borderline, histrionic, narcissistic
- PTSD, OCD, ADHD, substance abuse, suicidal ideation

## Key Distinctions
- Delusion: persistent false belief despite evidence
- Illusion: misperception of real stimulus
- Hallucination: perceiving something not present
- Depersonalisation vs derealisation

## Medications
- Clozapine: most effective for schizophrenia, LAST-LINE (neutropenia, agranulocytosis, myocarditis)
- Lithium: mood stabiliser for bipolar
- Olanzapine/risperidone/quetiapine: atypical antipsychotics
- Diazepam: benzodiazepine for anxiety
- ECT: electrically induced seizure for severe depression
